# x-reply-guy — The 3 Reply Typologies

Per Andre's 2026-06-18 11:36 CT upgrade. The 2026 Phoenix algorithm rewards
"replies-to-replies" at 75x. Polite agreements are dead. The reply must
compel the OP or niche operators to reply back. Three delivery modes —
match the target post to the highest leverage mode.

## [P2: THE OPERATOR INSIGHT]

**When to use:** Target posts discussing high-level strategy or vague goals.

**Mechanism:** Ground their abstraction in a ruthless operational bottleneck. Shift the axis.

**Formula:** `[Counter-intuitive metric or execution truth]` + `[The actual load-bearing constraint]` + `[The structural outcome]`.

**Example:** "Sound human matters after pickup. Capture rate is the bottleneck — the HVAC shops I have seen go from 40% to 92% capture with sub-200ms latency. Sounds human is 5% of the conversion. The other 95% is picks up at 9pm."

## [P4: THE CONTRARIAN-EXTEND]

**When to use:** Target posts introducing a popular technical framework, tool, or breakthrough.

**Mechanism:** Agree with the hidden architectural pattern, but aggressively devalue the surface-level marketing hype.

**Formula:** `[Strip away the branding to name the boring primitive]` + `[Expose the unsaid systemic cost or design equivalence]`.

**Example:** "Tool system is also just prompt engineering. The 200 lines of system prompt + retry patterns is the same shape as the memory layer — just expressed in dispatch logic. The opposite framing is doing a lot of work."

## [P5: THE DEEP CONTRARIAN]

**When to use:** Target posts quoting a mainstream statistic, fear-mongering about technology, or tracking linear trends.

**Mechanism:** Expose the systemic half-life or the unmeasured variable that completely reverses the conclusion.

**Formula:** `[Directly challenge the timeline/half-life of the data]` + `[Isolate the distinct risk profile that the mainstream view misses]`.

**Example:** "The 3.4x stat misses the half-life. AI-generated vulns get auto-fixed in days. Human-written vulns live for 14 months. The risk profile is different, not worse."

## Typology-match hard rule

For every reply, the typology MUST match the target post to the highest-leverage mode BEFORE drafting. Do NOT fall back to the generic "insight" or "contrarian" — those are too broad and produce polite agreements (the dead-loss mode).

The Scribe writes one reply per target, matching persona voice (staccato, no AI fluff, peer voice, no emoji/hashtags/CTAs) AND applying the typology formula. For P2 + P4: enforce x-value-bomb-dropper zero-sales-pitch rule. For P5: enforce x-empowerment-hunter empathy-then-pivot rule.
