### Direct Substack gating — step-by-step for non-technical use (Dre polish flag)

> **If you've never set up a Substack gated download before, this is the exact path.** Total time: ~10 minutes. No code required.

**Step 1 — Export the PDF**

1. Open `assets/lead-magnets/5-biomarkers-guide.md` in any markdown editor (Obsidian, VS Code, even TextEdit)
2. Copy all content from `# The 5 Biomarkers...` through the final `*Last updated:...*` line
3. Paste into a free online Markdown-to-PDF converter (e.g., md2pdf.netlify.app, markdowntopdf.com, or use Pandoc locally: `pandoc 5-biomarkers-guide.md -o 5-biomarkers-guide.pdf`)
4. Verify the PDF renders correctly: cover page, 5 sections, tracking tables visible, compliance footer at end

**Step 2 — Host the PDF**

1. Upload the PDF to your Substack media library (Substack → Posts → New Post → Attachment → Upload PDF)
2. Copy the resulting public URL
3. Alternative: upload to a free CDN (Cloudflare R2 free tier, Bunny.net free tier, or Google Drive with public link)

**Step 3 — Create the landing page post**

1. In Substack, create a new post
2. Set visibility to **"Free"** (so anyone can land on it and opt-in)
3. Paste the content of `5-biomarkers-landing-page.md` into the post body
4. Where it says `[** YOUR EMAIL **]   [** SEND ME THE PDF **]`, replace with the actual Substack subscribe embed code (Substack generates this automatically when you toggle "Subscribe" on for the post)
5. After the form, add a download link: `[Download the PDF]({{your_PDF_URL}})`
6. Publish

**Step 4 — Configure the welcome email**

1. Substack → Settings → Emails → Welcome email
2. Paste the body of `5-biomarkers-email-day0.md` (the section between `## Email body` and `## Compliance footer`)
3. Set the subject line to: `Your 5 Biomarkers PDF is inside`
4. Set the pre-header to: `Plus what to expect from Dose of Proof over the next 2 weeks`
5. Attach the PDF (optional — link is more reliable)
6. Save

**Step 5 — Verify end-to-end**

1. Open the landing page in a private browser window
2. Enter a test email address
3. Check that the welcome email arrives within 60 seconds
4. Verify the PDF download link works
5. Check that the "I'm still in this process" brand anchor appears in the welcome email

**Done.** Your lead magnet is live. Threads from the June 27 → July 10 calendar can now CTA to this page.
