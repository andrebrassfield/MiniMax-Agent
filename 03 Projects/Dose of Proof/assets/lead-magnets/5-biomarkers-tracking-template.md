**Daily protocol reminder:**

- **Morning HRV:** same app, same position, before caffeine, before screens
- **Guarding score (1-10):** how stuck and tense the skin and muscles feel today
- **Flushing (#):** count of flushing episodes today
- **Heat (#):** count of heat-intolerance episodes today
- **Sleep (1-10):** how rested you feel on waking
- **Notable events:** new food, new environment, new stressor, supplement change, adjustment, anything that might explain today's pattern

## Quick Reference: Red / Yellow / Green Flags

| Biomarker | 🔴 Red flag | 🟡 Yellow flag | 🟢 Green zone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning HRV | Sustained <30 (work with physician on full workup) | Bouncing 30-50s without trend (unstable but recovering — keep tracking) | Stable 50s+ with mild variation (vagal tone functional) |
| TyTron pattern | Severe asymmetry with sharp pattern breaks | Mild asymmetry with slow improvement | Near-symmetric patterns that hold between visits |
| Flare tryptase | 2x+ above baseline + elevated urine mediators | 1.5x above baseline + mild urine elevation | Within 1.2x of baseline + normal urine markers |
| Guarding score + blood markers | High scores (7+) + elevated CRP (active inflammation) | Moderate (4-6) + normal blood markers (settling) | Low (1-3) + normal blood markers (stable) |
| Vitamin D + Magnesium | D <20 ng/mL or RBC Mg below reference (significant deficiency) | D 20-30 or RBC Mg at low end (suboptimal) | D 40-60 or RBC Mg optimal (foundation solid) |
