- **X-rays** — clear craniocervical instability at C1-C2. Loss of normal curve. The upper neck was irritating my vagus nerve.

### The biomarker framework I built

After the imaging, I needed a way to track whether interventions were actually moving the needle. I built a 5-biomarker framework:

1. **Morning HRV** — vagus brake gauge. Free phone app, before caffeine. From chaotic low 30s-40s to consistent mid-50s+.

2. **TyTron paraspinal thermal pattern** — autonomic infrared scan. Direct visual feedback on whether the mechanical driver is being addressed. The lines straighten as the neck stabilizes.

3. **Tryptase + urine MCAS mediators** (during/after flare) — N-methylhistamine, LTE4, prostaglandin D2. Objective confirmation that mast cells are over-firing because the vagus brake is off.

4. **ESR + CRP + symptoms tracking** — inflammatory markers + daily "guarding score" 1-10 + flushing/heat episodes. Connects the mechanical fix to the downstream inflammation.

5. **Vitamin D + magnesium status** — 25-OH Vitamin D + RBC magnesium. Terrain foundation. Ligament laxity, mast cell reactivity, vagal tone, muscle guarding — all worsened when these are low.
