## Slide 5 — BIOMARKER 3

**Title:** Tryptase + Urine MCAS Mediators

- Result: "This isn't just anxiety" — the mast cells were firing because the vagus brake was off

## Slide 7 — BIOMARKER 5

**Title:** Vitamin D + Magnesium Status

**Subtitle:** The Terrain Foundation

**Body:**

- Once in range: nervous system less reactive, adjustments held longer

## Carousel design notes

- **Dimensions:** 1080×1080 (square) or 1080×1350 (portrait — better LinkedIn engagement)
- **Color palette:** Dark navy (#0A1628 or similar), teal/cyan accent (#00B4D8), white text
- **Typography:** Sans-serif, large headlines, body text minimum 24pt
- **Consistency:** Match the PDF's visual style for brand recognition
- **Animation:** Optional — slides can fade-in or have subtle Ken Burns effect
