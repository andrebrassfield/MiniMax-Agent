---

**Tweet 2:**

The FDA moved BPC-157 to Category 2 in 2023.

Their stated concerns:
- **Immunogenicity risk** — the peptide might trigger immune reactions
- **Insufficient API characterization** — manufacturers can't reliably prove the compound is what they say it is
- **Impurity profile unclear** — what other molecules are in the vial?

This is a manufacturing + safety decision, not an efficacy decision.

---

**Tweet 3:**

The FDA did NOT say BPC-157 doesn't work.

They said: "We don't have enough data to know if it's safe when administered to humans, and we don't have enough manufacturing consistency to know what's actually in the vial."

These are different concerns.

**Tweet 4:**

What would Category 1 transition actually change?

If the FDA moves BPC-157 to Category 1:
- 503A compounding pharmacies could legally produce it for human prescriptions
- Board-certified physicians could prescribe it through their practice
- Telehealth platforms (operating within the law) could offer it through supervised care
- Supply chain through legitimate channels would expand
