---

**Tweet 2:**

First: Emideltide (DSIP — Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide).

DSIP is a nonapeptide first characterized in 1977 from rabbit cerebral venous blood during slow-wave sleep. It has been studied for sleep architecture, stress response, and pain modulation in animal models.

Mechanism class: neuromodulator peptide.

---

**Tweet 3:**

DSIP regulatory state:

Removed from Category 2 in April 2026. Same procedural move as the July 23 batch and the July 24 Semax entry.

The FDA will review DSIP for inclusion on the 503A Bulks List on July 24. Non-binding recommendation framework applies.

---

**Tweet 4:**

Second: Epitalon.

Epitalon is a synthetic tetrapeptide (Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly) originally characterized in pineal gland extract research. The PCAC will evaluate it as a substance against the same evidentiary bar as every other compound on the docket — manufacturing characterization, safety data, and indication-specific evidence.

The committee's job is substance review. Online reputation is not on the agenda.

---

**Tweet 5:**

Epitalon regulatory state:

Same as DSIP — removed from Category 2 in April 2026, under PCAC review July 24 for 503A Bulks List inclusion.

The longevity-claim reputation around Epitalon is what will generate most public attention. The committee's job is to assess manufacturing + safety + the actual evidence on the table. Those are different frames.
