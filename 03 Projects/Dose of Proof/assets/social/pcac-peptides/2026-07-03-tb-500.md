**Tweet 2:**

Background.

TB-500 is a synthetic version of Thymosin Beta-4, a naturally occurring peptide involved in actin sequestration and tissue regeneration. The PCAC docket on July 23 evaluates TB-500 specifically for the wound healing indication.

Human data is sparse. The committee will weigh what's in the published literature, not what's in the online conversation.

---

**Tweet 3:**

Regulatory state:

TB-500 was on the Category 2 bulk substances list and was removed in April 2026 — same procedural move as BPC-157 and KPV.

The same "manufacturing + safety, not efficacy" framing applies.

---

**Tweet 4:**

What's actually being evaluated July 23:

The PCAC will review available data on TB-500 for wound healing and vote on whether to recommend inclusion on the 503A Bulks List.

If recommended AND if FDA follows the recommendation, state-licensed 503A pharmacies could compound TB-500 under physician prescription.

That's the entire "yes" path.
