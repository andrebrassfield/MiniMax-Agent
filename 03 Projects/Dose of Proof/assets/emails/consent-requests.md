## Template A — Case Study: Mark (38, entrepreneur)

**To:** [mark.email]
**Subject:** Quick ask — would you be open to me sharing your story?
**Pre-header:** 5-minute read. Specific use case.

---

Hey Mark,

Hope you're doing well. Quick ask.

I'm putting together the first cohort of the Dose of Proof Protocol — a course that teaches the PCAC framework (biomarker-driven, upstream-first biohacking). It's launching in the next few weeks.

Your story would be one of the most valuable things I could include. Specifically, the 6-week arc — TyTron asymmetry → daily flushing dropping from 7-8 episodes to 1-2 → HRV improvement → the "skin stuck" feeling decreasing enough that you could sit through full workdays again.

I'd want to share it in two specific places:

1. **The course sales page** as one of 3 case studies in the "Proof" section (with your first name + age + role, no last name, no company unless you want it).
2. **Launch email #7** as a slightly longer version with the specific biomarker numbers.

I'm not asking for an endorsement. I'm asking for permission to share your numbers + your arc, with the framing "I'm the first case study, then these people ran the framework."

**If yes, two things I need:**

1. Written confirmation (a reply to this email is fine) that I can use your first name + age + role + the specific numbers from your case.
2. Your blessing on the exact copy before it ships. I'll send you the draft before it goes live.

**If no, or if you want to see the draft first without committing** — also fine. Reply with "send draft" and I'll send it before asking for final consent.

Either way, no pressure. The brand goes to market without your story if you prefer.

Thanks for being one of the people who actually ran this before it was a product. That matters more than the case study itself.

— Dre
