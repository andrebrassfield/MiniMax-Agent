> If the video recording slips further, ship the audio-only origin story as a podcast-style bridge. Keeps the Lived Protocol pillar active in the launch window.

### Format
- **Audio:** Solo narration (Dre's voice), 15-25 minutes
- **Distribution:** Substack (audio post embed) + podcast platforms (Spotify, Apple Podcasts) + YouTube (static image + audio)
- **Recording tool:** GarageBand / Audacity / Voice Memos (any decent mic)
- **Visual:** Static frame with title + Dre's initials OR animated waveform

### Script (use existing origin story script + audio adaptations)

**Source:** `assets/video/origin-story-script.md` (the 10-min video script)

**Audio adaptation notes:**
- Add audio-only transitions: "I'll pause here for a moment." / "Take a breath with me."
- Lengthen certain sections that work better in audio (the biomarker numbers section can breathe more)
- Add "if you're listening while driving, here's the takeaway" callouts
- Add chapter markers for podcast platforms:
  - 00:00 — Opening
  - 02:30 — The 7 months
  - 06:00 — The medical runaround
  - 09:00 — The data
  - 13:00 — The framework
  - 18:00 — The current state

### Distribution plan

**Day 1 (Wednesday Jul 16 — Day 23 of pre-launch):**
- Substack audio post (native embed)
- YouTube upload (static visual + audio)
- Podcast platforms: Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Google Podcasts (manual upload to each)

**Day 2-7:**
- Cut into 4 short vertical clips (60-90 sec each) for X / YouTube Shorts / IG Reels / TikTok
- Same repurposing rules as the video (see Part 3 below)

### Why this works
- Audio is faster to record than video (no lighting, no setup, no editing)
- Audio feels intimate + personal (good for the brand voice)
- Podcast platforms create a permanent home for the origin story
- Audio can be transcribed → text repurposing layer

### What it doesn't replace
- The video version is preferred (more engagement on social)
- The audio is the bridge — ship video later as a richer version

## Part 3 — Repurposing Rules (Every Long → 5-7 Short)

> The systematic rule: every long-form piece of content automatically generates 5-7 short-form derivatives. This is the engine that compounds the brand's reach without multiplying the work.

### The Rule (Universal)

**Every long-form piece generates the following derivative set:**

| # | Format | Length | Platform | Cadence After Long |
|---|--------|--------|----------|---------------------|
| 1 | **X thread (compressed)** | 8-12 tweets | X | Same day |
| 2 | **X standalone (key insight)** | 1 tweet | X | Day +1 |
| 3 | **LinkedIn post (professional angle)** | 200-400 words | LinkedIn | Day +2 |
| 4 | **LinkedIn carousel (visual)** | 8-10 slides | LinkedIn | Day +3 |
| 5 | **Substack excerpt (email)** | 200-300 words | Substack welcome / launch sequence | Day +4 |
| 6 | **Skool pinned post (community)** | 100-200 words | Skool | Day +5 |
| 7 | **Short vertical clip (if video exists)** | 30-90 sec | YouTube Shorts / IG Reels / TikTok | Day +6 |

**Time investment:** ~2-3 hours of editing/repurposing per long-form piece → 7 derivative assets across platforms

### Repurposing Template (for each derivative)

```
SOURCE: [Original long-form piece — Substack post / video / carousel / etc.]

EXTRACT:
- The single most-quotable line
- The 2-3 key data points / numbers
- The most provocative / contrarian claim
- The "how to do this yourself" action

DERIVATIVE 1 (X thread):
- Lead with the most provocative claim
- 8-12 tweets, each standalone
- End with CTA: https://doseofproof.substack.com/

DERIVATIVE 2 (X standalone):
- 1 tweet: the most-quotable line + link

DERIVATIVE 3 (LinkedIn post):
- Professional angle: how this applies to the operator/operator audience
- 200-400 words
- CTA in first comment: https://doseofproof.substack.com/

DERIVATIVE 4 (LinkedIn carousel):
- 8-10 slides, 1 key point per slide
- Visual style: dark navy + teal accent (matches PDF brand)
- CTA: https://doseofproof.substack.com/

DERIVATIVE 5 (Substack excerpt):
- 200-300 words excerpt of the original
- Send as email to non-subscribers or welcome email to new subscribers

DERIVATIVE 6 (Skool pinned post):
- 100-200 words
- Frame as community discussion: "Here's what I wrote this week. What does your data say?"

DERIVATIVE 7 (Short vertical clip):
- 30-90 sec, the most provocative moment
- Captions burned in
- CTA end card: https://doseofproof.substack.com/
```

### Repurposing Cadence

**Long-form piece publishes Tuesday:**
- Wednesday: X thread (Derivative 1)
- Thursday: X standalone (Derivative 2)
- Friday: LinkedIn post (Derivative 3) + Skool pinned post (Derivative 6)
- Saturday: LinkedIn carousel (Derivative 4)
- Monday: Substack excerpt email (Derivative 5)
- Tuesday (next week): Short vertical clip (Derivative 7)

**Distribution cycle: 7 days across 6 platforms per long-form piece.**

### Volume Math

**Per long-form piece per week:**
- 1 long-form
- 7 derivatives
- = 8 pieces of content per long-form

**Per month (assuming 1 long-form per week):**
- 4 long-form pieces
- 28 derivatives
- = 32 total pieces

**That's the 5-7 pieces/week target hit comfortably, with the long-form as the foundation.**
