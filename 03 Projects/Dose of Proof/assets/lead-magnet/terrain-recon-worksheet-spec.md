# Terrain Recon Worksheet — Lead Magnet #2 (Full Spec)

> The second lead magnet. Where the 5 Biomarkers PDF teaches "what to track," the Terrain Recon Worksheet teaches "what's driving your symptoms." Self-assessment → personal terrain map → PCAC framework entry point.

## The Big Idea

**5 Biomarkers PDF** = "what to track" (downstream outputs)

**Terrain Recon Worksheet** = "what's driving the symptoms" (upstream inputs)

Together they form a complete entry point: the worksheet identifies WHERE to look, the biomarker tracking shows whether interventions are moving the needle.

---

## The Worksheet Structure

### Section 1 — Mechanical Drivers (15 questions)

**Purpose:** Identify upper cervical instability, vagus nerve irritation, postural issues, etc.

**Sample questions:**

1. Have you ever had an X-ray, MRI, or imaging of your neck or spine? If yes, what was found? (multiple choice: no imaging / loss of cervical curve / disc issues / instability / other)
2. Do you experience any of the following in your neck, jaw, or upper back: (multi-select: clicking/popping / tightness / pain / limited range of motion / none)
3. Have you ever seen a chiropractor, osteopath, or upper cervical specialist? (yes / no)
4. If yes, what was the result? (open text)
5. Rate your posture awareness from 1-10 (scale)
6. Do you clench or grind your teeth at night? (yes / no / don't know)
7. Have you ever had a head injury, whiplash, or concussion? (yes / no)
8. If yes, when? (open text)
9. Do you experience headaches? (never / occasionally / weekly / daily)
10. Rate your "skin stuck" or fascia tightness from 1-10 (scale)
11. Have you noticed any asymmetry in your body (one shoulder higher, one hip rotated, etc.)? (yes / no)
12. Do you experience TMJ symptoms (jaw clicking, pain, limited opening)? (yes / no)
13. Have you ever been diagnosed with Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (EDS) or hypermobility? (yes / no / suspected)
14. Rate your morning neck stiffness from 1-10 (scale)
15. Do you find yourself unconsciously clenching or guarding your muscles? (yes / no / sometimes)

**Scoring (Section 1):**
- 0-4 yes answers: low mechanical driver signal
- 5-9 yes answers: moderate signal — consider imaging + upper cervical workup
- 10-15 yes answers: strong signal — priority workup recommended

---

### Section 2 — Vagal Tone + Autonomic Function (12 questions)

**Purpose:** Identify vagus nerve dysfunction, dysautonomia, autonomic symptoms.

**Sample questions:**

1. Do you experience any of the following (multi-select: heart rate variability changes / dizziness on standing / temperature dysregulation / exercise intolerance / GI symptoms that worsen with stress / none)
2. How would you rate your overall stress resilience from 1-10? (scale)
3. Do you experience "fight or flight" activation in situations that don't warrant it? (yes / no / sometimes)
4. How is your sleep quality? (1-10 scale)
5. Do you wake feeling rested? (yes / no / sometimes)
6. Have you been diagnosed with POTS, dysautonomia, or similar? (yes / no / suspected)
7. Do you experience anxiety or panic that comes out of nowhere? (yes / no / sometimes)
8. Rate your "wired but tired" feeling from 1-10 (scale)
9. Do you track HRV (heart rate variability)? (yes / no / don't know what that is)
10. If yes, is your morning HRV trending up, down, or stable? (up / down / stable / don't know)
11. Do you experience brain fog or cognitive slowing? (yes / no / sometimes)
12. Have you tried vagal breathing, cold exposure, or other vagal toning practices? (yes / no / tried but didn't stick)

**Scoring (Section 2):**
- 0-3 yes answers: low autonomic dysfunction signal
- 4-7 yes answers: moderate signal — start tracking HRV + work with physician on autonomic workup
- 8-12 yes answers: strong signal — priority autonomic workup recommended

---

### Section 3 — Environmental + Immune Drivers (15 questions)

**Purpose:** Identify mold, redox, mineral, mast cell, gut, and inflammatory drivers.

**Sample questions:**

1. Have you lived or worked in a building with known water damage or mold? (yes / no / not sure)
2. If yes, how long? (open text)
3. Have you been tested for mold mycotoxins? (yes / no)
4. Have you been diagnosed with MCAS (Mast Cell Activation Syndrome)? (yes / no / suspected)
5. Do you experience any of the following triggers: (multi-select: heat / cold / stress / foods / alcohol / exercise / fragrances / chemicals / none)
6. Do you experience flushing or hives without clear cause? (yes / no / sometimes)
7. Have you had your Vitamin D levels tested? (yes / no)
8. If yes, what was the level? (open text or numeric)
9. Have you had RBC magnesium tested? (yes / no)
10. Do you experience food sensitivities that have changed over time? (yes / no / haven't tracked)
11. Do you experience any GI symptoms (bloating, diarrhea, constipation, pain)? (yes / no)
12. Have you been tested for SIBO, candida, or other gut dysbiosis? (yes / no / yes — what was found)
13. Rate your inflammatory markers (hs-CRP, ESR) from 1-10 if you have them (open)
14. Have you tried an elimination diet? (yes / no / tried — what happened)
15. Do you feel worse in certain environments (moldy buildings, after travel, etc.)? (yes / no / not sure)

**Scoring (Section 3):**
- 0-4 yes answers: low environmental/immune driver signal
- 5-9 yes answers: moderate signal — consider environmental assessment + gut workup
- 10-15 yes answers: strong signal — priority workup recommended

---

### Section 4 — Symptom Inventory + Severity (12 questions)

**Purpose:** Quantify the symptom burden so the user can track changes over time.

**Symptoms to rate 1-10:**
1. Flushing episodes (frequency + severity)
2. Heat sensitivity
3. "Skin stuck" feeling / fascia tightness
4. Anxiety / panic
5. Brain fog / cognitive slowing
6. Fatigue
7. Sleep disturbance
8. GI symptoms (bloating, pain, etc.)
9. Joint or muscle pain
10. Numbness or tingling
11. Temperature dysregulation
12. Overall sense of "I don't feel like myself"

**Scoring (Section 4):**
- Sum of all 12 ratings → Symptom Burden Score (0-120)
- 0-30: low burden
- 31-60: moderate burden
- 61-90: high burden
- 91-120: severe burden

---

### Section 5 — Demographics + Context (5 questions)

**Purpose:** Stratify results + identify patterns.

1. Age range (under 18 / 18-29 / 30-44 / 45-59 / 60+)
2. Gender (M / F / non-binary / prefer not to say)
3. Years since symptoms started (under 1 / 1-3 / 3-5 / 5-10 / 10+)
4. Number of specialists seen for these symptoms (0 / 1-3 / 4-6 / 7+)
5. Have you been told "your labs are normal" by a doctor? (yes / no)

## The Scoring Logic (Decision Tree)

```
Section 1 (Mechanical) score:
  ≥10 → "Strong mechanical driver signal. Priority workup recommended. Start with imaging (X-rays, TyTron scans) + upper cervical specialist consultation."
  5-9 → "Moderate signal. Consider imaging if not already done. Talk to a chiropractor with upper cervical expertise."
  0-4 → "Lower signal. May not be the primary driver for your case."

Section 2 (Vagal) score:
  ≥8 → "Strong autonomic dysfunction signal. Priority HRV tracking + physician workup for dysautonomia."
  4-7 → "Moderate signal. Start daily HRV tracking. Consider autonomic workup."
  0-3 → "Lower signal."

Section 3 (Env/Immune) score:
  ≥10 → "Strong environmental/immune driver signal. Consider environmental assessment (mold, mycotoxins), gut workup, MCAS testing."
  5-9 → "Moderate signal. Consider gut workup, Vitamin D + magnesium testing, mast cell evaluation."
  0-4 → "Lower signal."

Combined scoring:
  Any 2+ sections at "strong signal" → "Your terrain suggests multiple interacting drivers. The PCAC framework was built for this complexity. The course teaches the operationalized approach."
  Any 1 section at "strong signal" → "Start with the highest-scoring section. The framework teaches you to address one upstream driver at a time."
  All sections at "low" → "Your symptoms may have a different driver pattern. Consider broader differential diagnosis with your physician."
```

## Compliance Architecture

### Mandatory footer on every output PDF + web form:

> "This worksheet is for educational and informational purposes only. It is not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. Always consult your physician before starting, changing, or stopping any protocol. The framework teaches you to map your own terrain; the protocol decisions are yours and your doctor's."

### Banned language in the worksheet itself:
- ❌ "You have X" / "You suffer from Y" (diagnostic framing)
- ❌ "You should do X" (prescriptive)
- ❌ "Cures" / "treats" / "heals" (medical claims)

### Required language:
- ✅ "Your responses suggest..." (observational)
- ✅ "Consider talking to your physician about..." (referral)
- ✅ "The PCAC framework may help you..." (educational)
