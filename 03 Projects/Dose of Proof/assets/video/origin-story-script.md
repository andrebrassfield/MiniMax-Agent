# Origin Story Video Script — 10 minutes

## Setup notes for Dre

**Format:** Direct-to-camera, single take or 2 takes max. Phone or webcam is fine — the rawness IS the brand.

**Lighting:** Natural light from one side (window). No ring light needed unless you want it.

**Audio:** Quiet room, phone close. Lavalier mic if you have one.

**Background:** Plain wall, no clutter. Or your workspace. Authenticity > polish.

**Wardrobe:** Whatever you'd wear to a coffee shop. No suit. No "YouTuber" costume.

**Energy:** Calm but direct. Not motivational. Not theatrical. Think "smart friend telling you the worst year of their life over coffee."

**Length:** 10 minutes is the target. Don't rush. If you hit 12 minutes, that's fine — cut in editing.

## TIMESTAMP 0:10 — The setup

**[Direct to camera. Calm, direct.]**

I'm 30 years old. I have a philosophy degree. For most of my adult life I've tried to live as stoically as possible.

I thought I was handling things.

Then the last seven months showed me I was wrong.

**[Pause. 1 second.]**

This video is what happened. The 7 months that broke me. The doctors who couldn't help. The framework I had to build for myself.

And where I am now — which is still in it, but with the data to know what's working.

## TIMESTAMP 0:45 — The onset

**[Same energy. No drama. Just facts.]**

It started with flushing that came out of nowhere.

Heat sensitivity so bad I couldn't be in normal rooms.

Then the pain — deep, burning inflammation in my face, neck, back, shoulders, stomach, hips. The worst part was this thing I've started calling the "skin stuck" feeling.

**[Optional: B-roll of your hands, your skin, the flare-up. Or just hands on the desk.]**

My skin and fascia would bunch and get tight. My body would subconsciously recruit every other muscle to protect the unstable areas. That created knots and trigger points everywhere.

I was guarding constantly. I didn't even realize it until it became my new normal.

---

## TIMESTAMP 2:00 — The breaking point

**[Voice tightens. Not performative. Just honest.]**

I had crushing anxiety.

Brain fog so thick I had to stop taking Adderall. I was on it for ADHD. The brain fog was worse than the ADHD it was treating.

I started disappearing from my own life.

I was distant with friends. Slow to reply. Canceling plans. I felt like I was failing as a friend and as a man.

**[Pause.]**

My dad died fast — stage 4 lung cancer diagnosed in August 2018, gone by November. He'd quit smoking the day I was born and stayed clean for 21 years.

We lived in the same house with hidden toxic mold for about four years before he got sick.

I now believe that mold, plus whatever genetic terrain we shared, set the stage for what eventually hit me.

---

## TIMESTAMP 3:30 — The medical runaround

**[Energy shifts to frustration. Real, not performed.]**

I went to 4 specialists.

The first told me it was anxiety. The second told me it was fibromyalgia. The third said mast cell activation. The fourth said "just stress" and prescribed an SSRI.

Each one gave me a different label and a different pill. None of them connected the dots.

I was disappearing, and the medical system was profiting from keeping me fragmented.

**[Pause.]**

That's the part that broke something in me. Not the symptoms. The realization that the system isn't set up to fix this.

I had been living in a vicious loop:

Unstable neck → vagus irritation → mast cells firing → more inflammation and guarding → more instability.

Most doctors wanted to treat one symptom at a time. I was done with that.
