## Production notes

**Total runtime of source:** 10 minutes (1 recording session)
**Number of clips cut:** 4
**Total runtime of clips:** ~4-5 minutes (under the 3-videos / 5-hour limit because these are CUTS from one source, not separate AI-generated videos)

**Captions:** All clips need burned-in captions (80%+ of social video is watched without sound). Use the exact dialogue from the script.

**On-screen text:** Minimal. The "hook" in the first 3 seconds + the "end card" at the end. Don't overload.

**Vertical format:** 9:16 aspect ratio. Film Dre in vertical framing from the start, OR re-frame in editing (top-center crop).

**Posting schedule (after the long-form video is live):**
- Day 1 (Monday): Clip 1 (origin hook)
- Day 3 (Wednesday): Clip 2 (specialists)
- Day 5 (Friday): Clip 3 (PCAC intro)
- Day 7 (Sunday): Clip 4 (still in it)
- Then cycle: repeat weekly on different platforms

**Cross-post to:**
- YouTube Shorts

- TikTok
- X video (attached to standalone tweets)

**Engagement plan:**
- Reply to every comment in first 2 hours with substantive thought
- Pin Clip 1 (origin hook) to X profile during launch month

**Anti-patterns:**
- No "link in bio" if the actual destination is unclear (always end card with specific PDF URL)

- No "cures" or "treats" — even in caption text
