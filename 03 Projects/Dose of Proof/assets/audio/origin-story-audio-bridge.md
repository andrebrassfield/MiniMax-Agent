# Audio Origin Story Bridge — 18 minute script

> Read aloud. Direct to mic. Quiet room. Phone close. The rawness is the brand.

**Audio cues legend:**
- **[PAUSE]** = 1-2 second silence (let it land)
- **[LONG PAUSE]** = 2-3 second silence (let the moment breathe)
- **[BREATHE]** = audible breath, intentional
- **[EMPHASIS]** = punch the word slightly, no shouting
- **[LOWER]** = quieter, more intimate
- **[DRIVE CALLOUT]** = "if you're listening while driving, here's the takeaway" pattern
- **[CHAPTER]** = chapter marker; pause 1 second after for clean edit

## [CHAPTER] 02:30 — The Seven Months

**[PAUSE]**

It started with flushing that came out of nowhere.

Heat sensitivity so bad I couldn't sit in normal rooms. Normal air conditioning. Normal body heat from another person in the room. Normal sunlight through a window.

**[BREATHE]**

Then the pain. Deep, burning inflammation in my face, neck, back, shoulders, stomach, hips. The worst part was this thing I've started calling the "skin stuck" feeling.

**[LOWER]**

My skin and fascia would bunch and get tight. My body would subconsciously recruit every other muscle to protect the unstable areas. That created knots and trigger points everywhere. My shoulders would lock. My jaw would clench. My lower back would guard.

I was guarding constantly. I didn't even realize it until it became my new normal.

**[PAUSE]**

I had crushing anxiety.

Brain fog so thick I had to stop taking Adderall. I was on it for ADHD. The brain fog was worse than the ADHD it was treating. I'd lose sentences mid-thought. I'd stare at my screen for minutes at a time, unable to start.

I started disappearing from my own life.

**[EMPHASIS]** Distant with friends. Slow to reply. Canceling plans. I felt like I was failing as a friend and as a man.

**[LONG PAUSE]**

My dad died fast.

Stage four lung cancer, diagnosed August 2018, gone by November. He'd quit smoking the day I was born and stayed clean for twenty-one years.

We lived in the same house with hidden toxic mold for about four years before he got sick.

**[LOWER]** I now believe that mold — plus whatever genetic terrain we shared — set the stage for what eventually hit me.
