# Audio Origin Story — Recording-Day Package

> The full script is in `assets/audio/origin-story-audio-bridge.md`. This is the **recording-day cheat sheet** — print this, tape it next to your mic, hit record.

## Pre-recording checklist (T-30 minutes)

- [ ] Phone / mic charged, tested (record 30 sec + playback)
- [ ] Quiet room (close windows, AC off, phone on silent)
- [ ] Water bottle within reach (you'll need it)
- [ ] Standing or sitting up straight (better breath support)
- [ ] Coffee optional but not required
- [ ] Read this cheat sheet once aloud before recording (muscle memory)
- [ ] **Optional:** open the full script on your phone in case you blank on a line

## Chapter-by-chapter timing + emphasis notes

### CHAPTER 0:00 — Opening (~1 min)

**Text:** "I'm thirty years old. I have a philosophy degree..."

**Target time:** 0:00 - 1:00 (60 seconds)

**Emphasis:**
- "I'm thirty years old" — calm, factual, not dramatic
- "I thought I was handling things" — slight pause after "handling things"
- "Then the last seven months showed me I was wrong" — quiet, honest, NOT theatrical

**Energy:** Calm. Direct. Smart friend telling you over coffee.

**Pace:** Slow. Don't rush the setup.

---

### CHAPTER 2:30 — The Seven Months (~3.5 min)

**Text:** "It started with flushing that came out of nowhere..."

**Target time:** 2:30 - 6:00 (210 seconds)

**Emphasis:**
- "flushing that came out of nowhere" — matter-of-fact
- "Heat sensitivity so bad I couldn't sit in normal rooms" — pause after "normal rooms"
- "the 'skin stuck' feeling" — quote the phrase with air quotes in your voice
- "My body would subconsciously recruit every other muscle to protect the unstable areas" — slower pace, this is the key insight
- "I was guarding constantly" — pause after this line
- "My dad died fast." — drop your voice. Let it land.
- "He'd quit smoking the day I was born and stayed clean for twenty-one years" — slight pause after "twenty-one years"

**Drive callout (mid-chapter, around 4:30):** "If you're listening while driving — here's the takeaway: when you have multi-system illness, each specialist treats their slice. Nobody connects the upstream drivers."

**Energy:** Build from matter-of-fact → emotional (the dad section) → back to direct

**Pace:** Medium. Let the key phrases breathe.

---

### CHAPTER 6:00 — The Medical Runaround (~3 min)

**Text:** "I went to four specialists..."

**Target time:** 6:00 - 9:00 (180 seconds)

**Emphasis:**
- "The first told me it was anxiety. The second told me it was fibromyalgia. The third said mast cell activation. The fourth said 'just stress' and prescribed an SSRI." — count on your fingers, give each diagnosis its own beat
- "None of them connected the dots." — quiet, not angry
- "I had five prescription bottles on my counter and zero connection between them." — this is the visual anchor
- "The system profits from keeping people fragmented." — this is the brand-defining line. Slow down. Let it land.

**Drive callout (around 7:30):** "If you're driving — here's the takeaway: when you have multi-system illness, each specialist treats their slice. Nobody connects the upstream drivers. You become the integrator, or nobody does."

**Energy:** Frustration → resignation → quiet resolve

**Pace:** Faster through the specialist list, slower through the system critique.

---

### CHAPTER 9:00 — The Data (~4 min)

**Text:** "In April 2026 — about seven months in — I finally got real data..."

**Target time:** 9:00 - 13:00 (240 seconds)

**Emphasis:**
- "I finally got real data" — relief, slight smile
- "Cervical X-rays showed clear craniocervical instability at C1-C2" — clinical, precise
- "TyTron infrared scans" — most listeners won't know this term. Pause after. Let them look it up if they want.
- "I fit the classic picture of suspected hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome." — slow down. This is the moment of recognition.
- The loop counting — count on your fingers, give each loop element its own beat. This is the framework's pivot.

**Drive callout (around 11:30):** "If you're driving — here's the framework's pivot: stop chasing symptoms. Start mapping the upstream terrain. The biomarker framework asks: what's the most upstream driver I can identify with data? Treat that first."

**Energy:** Build from relief → clinical → resolve

**Pace:** Faster through clinical findings, slower through the framework pivot.

---

### CHAPTER 13:00 — The Framework (~2 min)

**Text:** "I found a Blair upper cervical specialist..."

**Target time:** 13:00 - 15:00 (120 seconds)

**Emphasis:**
- "He took the time to actually review all my imaging instead of rushing an adjustment." — this is the contrast to the 4 specialists. Slight emphasis on "actually."
- "I built the framework I call PCAC." — drop your voice slightly. This is the brand-defining moment.
- "Proof-Centered Approach to Craniocervical + Autoimmune Chaos" — spell it out clearly. Listeners will be writing this down.
- "Show me the data. Show me the before and after. Prove it's working or change the approach." — this is the framework's mantra. Slow down. Each phrase its own beat.
- "Same acronym. Two meanings. I use both." — quick, almost offhand. The dual meaning is a feature, not a surprise.

**Energy:** Calm, steady. The framework is presented, not performed.

**Pace:** Slow. Let the mantra land.

---

### CHAPTER 15:00 — The Numbers (~1.5 min)

**Text:** "Here's what the data looked like..."

**Target time:** 15:00 - 16:30 (90 seconds)

**Emphasis:**
- "Here's what the data looked like." — quiet. Setting up the numbers.
- All four biomarker readings — calm, clinical, specific. The numbers ARE the point.
- "I'm not recovered. I'm not cured." — punch these. The disavowal is the differentiator.
- "I'm tracking data that's trending in the right direction" — slow down.
- "The numbers move. They don't arrive at a finish line." — the brand anchor. Let it land.

**Energy:** Quiet, factual, slightly humble.

**Pace:** Slow. The numbers don't need performance.

---

### CHAPTER 16:30 — The Brand (~1.5 min)

**Text:** "I'm building this brand — Dose of Proof..."

**Target time:** 16:30 - 18:00 (90 seconds)

**Emphasis:**
- "I'm building this brand — Dose of Proof" — first mention of the brand name. Land it.
- "because the system profits from keeping people sick and fragmented" — the brand-defining line. Slow. Let it breathe.
- "I'm not a doctor. I don't prescribe." — explicit disavowal. Punch it.
- "I'm the curator." — simple. Direct.
- "If that's you — the people who got the five diagnoses and the five prescriptions and the five co-pays and zero connection between them — the five biomarkers that actually moved the needle for me are free in a PDF." — the lead magnet pitch. Conversational, not salesy.
- "Substack. Doseofproof.substack.com. The link is in the show notes." — clear, slow. Spell the URL.
- "Same day as the FDA PCAC meeting. Same day as the regulatory moment. Both happening on July twenty-third." — date-specific. Listeners should mark their calendar.
- "I'll be transparent with you the whole way. Talk soon." — quiet, direct. End.

**Energy:** Resolve. Quiet conviction.

**Pace:** Slow through the brand-defining line, conversational through the CTA.

---

### END — Silence

**Target:** 3-5 seconds of silence after "Talk soon." before stopping the recording.

Don't fill the silence. The silence is the brand.

---

## Total target: 18 minutes

If you hit 17 minutes, that's fine. If you hit 20 minutes, that's also fine. Don't rush to hit 18 — better to breathe than to sprint.

If you hit 25 minutes, that's the upper bound. Cut in editing.
