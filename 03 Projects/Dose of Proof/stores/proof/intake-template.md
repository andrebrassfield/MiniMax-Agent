## Compliance notes (Dre's eyes only — don't send)

- Every question is structured to capture **biomarker trends and operational data**, not therapeutic-outcome language
- The "Your words" section (Section 7) is where therapeutic-outcome language is most likely to creep in — Dre will need to redact during draft production if the person writes "I recovered from X" or "this peptide cured me"
- Section 8's identity + data scope is critical — every published story must match the consent scope exactly, no scope creep
- The withdrawal right acknowledgment in Section 8 is mandatory — no publication without it
- The 7-10 day draft turnaround is a soft SLA — set realistic expectations
