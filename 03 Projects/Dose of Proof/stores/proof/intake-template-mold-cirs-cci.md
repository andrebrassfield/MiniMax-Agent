- Sinus congestion / chronic sinusitis

- Skin issues (rashes, itching, flushing)
- Heat intolerance / temperature dysregulation
- Mood changes (anxiety, depression, irritability)
- Sleep disruption
- Hormonal disruption (cycle changes, libido changes)
- New or worsening autoimmune presentations

## Why this intake is mold/CIRS-specific

The generic intake captures the basics. This variant captures the upstream terrain data the brand's content cluster is built around:

1. **Mold exposure context** — so the case study can accurately describe the environmental driver
2. **CIRS-specific labs** — the markers most practitioners in this space actually use (C4a, TGF-beta-1, MMP-9, VIP, MSH, HLA-DR)
3. **Upper cervical + autonomic assessment** — the upstream mechanical driver the brand's framework emphasizes
4. **Connective tissue assessment** — the genetic terrain that makes mold exposure more likely to manifest as multi-system illness
5. **Protocol elements** — to describe what the person tried (without recommending)
6. **Mold-specific compliance scoping** — because the mold/CIRS space has higher regulatory scrutiny (especially around specific dosing recommendations)

The intake respects the brand's compliance posture: "I'm not a doctor. I don't prescribe. The framework teaches you to read your own data; the protocol decisions are yours and your doctor's."
