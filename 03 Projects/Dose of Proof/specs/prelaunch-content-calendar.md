## Pre-Launch Calendar Notes

**Email list is the #1 KPI.** Every piece of content drives to one of these opt-ins:
- The Substack (primary list for the brand)
- The Skool free tier (community engagement)
- The LinkedIn follow (professional credibility)

**Content mix principle:** 60% educational, 20% origin story, 10% community engagement, 10% promotional. The educational content is the lead magnet + the PCAC framework.

**The FDA PCAC meeting is the catalyst on launch day.** Coverage starts July 23 morning, same day the cart opens. Both PCACs (FDA + brand) peak together.

**Repurposing chain:** Each piece of long-form content (Substack post, YouTube video) feeds 2-4 short-form pieces (X tweets, LinkedIn carousels, Skool posts). One hour of creation → 4 hours of distribution.

**Engagement rules:**
- Reply to every comment in first 2 hours (Dre's voice, not generic)
- Pin the lead magnet thread to X profile during Weeks 1-2
- Pin the cart-open announcement to X profile during launch week
- The LinkedIn Post 1 ("I'm still in this process") stays as the pinned LinkedIn post

**What to NOT do:**
- No fake urgency outside the founding-member 200 cap
- No "limited spots" copy
- No fake countdown timers
- No manufactured "live launch" energy

The 90-day calendar in `business-model-v1.md` Section 7 is the broader plan; this calendar is the pre-launch slice.
