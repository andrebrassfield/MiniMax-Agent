## 0. Voice Calibration — Dre's Voice

### The 7 universal voice rules (applied to Dre)

| # | Rule | Dre's expression |
|---|------|------------------|
| 1 | Specific > generic | "I ran 250mcg of BPC-157 for 6 weeks" not "I took some peptides" |
| 2 | Short + medium sentences | Mix of 6-12 word punches with 18-22 word explanations |
| 3 | Active voice dominant | "I ran the math" not "the math was run" |
| 4 | No filler | Cut every "in today's world", "leverage", "going forward" |
| 5 | Operator > brand voice | Dre speaking, not "Dose of Proof™" speaking |
| 6 | Builder not marketer | "Here's how it works" not "Unlock the power" |
| 7 | Honest about limitations | "This won't work if you don't run your own bloodwork" |

### Dre-specific voice (encoded from the source)

**Tone:** First-person, contrarian, evidence-anchored, builder-proud, contrarian-not-cynical.

**Vocabulary anchors:**
- "Citizen scientist" (not "researcher", not "guru")
- "Recon" (peptide reconstitution)
- "Biomarkers" / "bloodwork" / "panels"
- "My doctor" / "my physician" (when referring to supervised care)
- "The math" / "the framework" / "the protocol"
- "My body, not yours" (the compliance framing)
- "Read the literature" / "look at the data"
- "Affiliated partner" (not "sponsor" or "deal")

**Banned vocabulary (compliance + voice):**
- "Cure" / "treat" / "heal" (compliance — see business-model-v1.md Section 2.2)
- "Prescribe" / "diagnose" (compliance — operator is not a physician)
- "Secret" / "they don't want you to know" (banned hype)
- "Game-changing" / "revolutionary" / "breakthrough" (banned hype)
- "Unleash" / "unlock" (banned)
- "Synergy" / "leverage" / "value-add" (corporate sludge)
- "We" (when solo — use "I" or "Dose of Proof" as the brand)

**Tone shifts by surface:**

| Surface | Tone | Length |
|---------|------|--------|
| Sales page | Confident, mechanism-forward, proof-heavy | 2,000-2,500 words |
| Email | Conversational, 1:1 letter voice | 200-500 words |
| Landing page | Direct, single-message | 300-600 words |
| Ad copy | Punchy, outcome-first | 10-30 words |
| Sales call | Listening-heavy, story-driven | n/a (live) |

**The voice test:** would I say this sentence to a smart friend over coffee? If yes, ship. If no, rewrite.
