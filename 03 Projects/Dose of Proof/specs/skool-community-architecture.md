## Moderation Rules (Tier 2 + Tier 3)

### The 5 Unbreakable Rules (carryover from business-model-v1.md)

1. **No sourcing language** — no peptide supplier names (Peptide Sciences, Onyx, Swiss Chems, etc.), no RUO labeling
2. **No MLM references** — no "refer 3 friends, get X"
3. **No un-redacted testimonials** — therapeutic-outcome language moderated
4. **No "cure / treat / heal" claims** — for self or others
5. **Substantiation standard** — every health claim cites peer-reviewed study, regulatory filing, named expert, or personal biomarker panel

### Operational moderation

**Trigger:** Member posts something that violates the 5 rules
**Action:**
1. Post is auto-flagged (keywords: "cures" / "buy from" / "this guy" + supplier name / "join my affiliate")
2. Dre (or moderator) DMs the member with: "Hey, your post was flagged because [specific rule]. The brand's compliance posture is [reference]. Can you repost without [specific element]?"
3. If the member is responsive: repost the cleaned version. If not: post stays flagged, doesn't appear in main feed.
4. Repeat violations: temporary mute (7 days), then permanent removal if continued

### Member-on-member moderation
- Members can flag other members' posts (report button)
- Dre (or moderator) reviews flagged content within 24 hours
- Aggressive / disrespectful content is removed
- Disagreements about protocols are allowed (with substantive data); personal attacks are not

### Compliance-specific language in moderation
When moderating, Dre uses his voice: "Hey, that post got flagged because of [specific rule]. I'm not trying to be the language police — I want this community to be useful for everyone. Can you repost without [X]? The framework is [Y]."

## Substack Integration (Avoiding Objective Intent Exposure)

### The structural problem
The Objective Intent Doctrine treats brand-storefront-community as an integrated ecosystem. If the Skool community hosts sourcing discussions, the brand's Substack + courses + SaaS become "evidence of intended use."

### The architectural solution

**Tier 1 (Free) — open public content, no sourcing**
- Public posts are read-only
- Comments are moderated for sourcing language
- No DMs between members in Tier 1
- Tier 1 is functionally an extension of the Substack (read-mostly)

**Tier 2 (Inner Circle) — moderated private community, sourcing moderated**
- Members can post protocols + questions + biomarker data
- All posts moderated for sourcing language + therapeutic claims
- DMs available but flagged for sourcing
- Tier 2 is a moderated education community

**Tier 3 (Mastermind) — vetted cohort, deeper discussion**
- Members are vetted via application (8 questions)
- Deeper discussion allowed (still no sourcing)
- 1:1 protocol review with Dre (still no prescribing — Dre's review is framework-based, not medical)
- Tier 3 is a curated cohort

### What NEVER happens in any tier
- ❌ No "where do you buy [compound]" discussions (auto-flag + redirect to vetted telehealth partners)
- ❌ No "this peptide is best for X" claims (moderated — only Dre can speak with that authority)
- ❌ No "use this supplier" recommendations
- ❌ No MLM recruitment
- ❌ No "I cured my [condition] with [compound]" claims (the brand's compliance posture forbids therapeutic-outcome language for compounds)

### What happens instead
- Members share biomarker data + protocol notes (compliant format)
- Members ask questions about recon math + dosing + biomarker interpretation
- Members discuss their physician's recommendations
- Members share their own experience in operational terms (HRV, sleep, etc.) — not therapeutic-outcome terms
