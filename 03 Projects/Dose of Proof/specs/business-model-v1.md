## 2. Compliance Architecture (NON-NEGOTIABLE — locks in before revenue)

The source identified 4 FDA "traps" (algorithmic cross-selling, SEO metadata, un-redacted testimonials, community-storefront integration). The redesign treats these as **architectural constraints**, not rules-of-thumb. Every system below is designed to structurally avoid them.

### 2.1 — The 5 unbreakable rules

1. **"My body, not yours"** — never recommend. Always narrate. ("When my gut was destroyed, I looked into the data on BPC-157…" not "You should take BPC-157 for your gut.")
2. **No un-redacted testimonials** — if a comment claims a therapeutic outcome, delete or it becomes corporate labeling.
3. **No gray-market sourcing** — no links to research-chem suppliers. Period. Affiliate programs from Peptide Sciences, Onyx, Swiss Chems, Limitless Life = declined.
4. **Ecosystem separation** — owned channels (Substack, Skool, app) keep consumer discussion. No storefront in the same funnel. Affiliate links go to licensed telehealth only.
5. **Substantiation standard** — every health claim cites either (a) a peer-reviewed study, (b) a regulatory filing, (c) a named expert, or (d) a personal biomarker panel labeled as such.

### 2.2 — Language templates (banned phrasings → required phrasings)

| Banned | Required |
|--------|----------|
| "You should take X" | "When I dealt with Y, I looked into X" |
| "Cures / treats / heals" | "May support / associated with / in the literature" |
| "Limited supply" (when untrue) | Real cohort caps or none |
| "Doctors don't want you to know" | "Most 15-minute visits don't cover this" |
| "Secret method" | "Here's the data I cross-referenced" |
| "Game-changing" | Specific outcome metric |
| "Breakthrough" | Specific mechanism + source |
| First-person plural "we recommend" (when solo) | "I" / "my" |
| Affiliate link to RUO supplier | Affiliate link to 503A pharmacy via telehealth platform |

### 2.3 — Testimonial moderation policy

- All testimonials moderated, none published automatically
- Therapeutic-outcome language → redacted or rejected
- "It worked for me" allowed; "it will work for you" rejected
- All published testimonials carry a footer: "Individual results vary. This is not medical advice."
- Skool / Discord: pinned post with the moderation rules + 1-click report

### 2.4 — Link-in-bio stack (the only allowed destinations)

```
Layer 1 (free):
- Substack (`https://doseofproof.substack.com/`) — subdomain URL, owned
- Podcast (doseofproof.com/podcast or wherever hosted)
- YouTube channel
- Skool community (free tier preview)

Layer 2 (paid, owned):
- Skool full community (skool.com/doseofproof)
- Course platform (Teachable / Kajabi / Skool classroom)
- SaaS app (doseofproof.app — freemium)
- Book store (Amazon KDP / Gumroad)

Layer 3 (affiliate, vetted):
- Marek Health (4% / $20 per checkout)
- Lifeforce (16% per subscription, recurring)
- Thorne Ambassador (10-20% tiered)
- BiOptimizers (16-30%)
- InsideTracker (tiered, athletic focus)

Layer 4 (NEVER):
- Research-chem suppliers (Peptide Sciences, Onyx, Swiss Chems, Limitless Life)
- MLM structures (MAKE Wellness and similar)
- Unlicensed telehealth / gray-market telehealth
```

### 2.5 — Compliance audit cadence

- **Weekly:** review published content for banned phrasings
- **Monthly:** audit all affiliate links (any new partner reviewed before activation)
- **Quarterly:** full compliance review with FDA regulatory updates (especially post-PCAC meeting July 2026)
- **Event-triggered:** any FDA warning letter to a partner → 24h link audit

## 3. Income Stream Architecture — 15 streams

### Tier 1 — Owned assets (the moat)

These are the streams that compound. They're the difference between "creator" and "business." Highest priority to build.

**Architecture decision (locked 2026-06-23 20:04 CT):** The brand operates **two distinct tools**, not one combined SaaS:

- **health-tracker** (existing CLI) — free/open-source, symptoms + meds + daily metrics + guarding scores + HRV. Distributed to the email list. Builds trust + proof ecosystem. *Not* the SaaS offer.
- **Dose Calc** (planned SaaS, stream C below) — paid, peptide reconstitution math + dose history + multi-vial tracking. The paid SaaS offer.

Rationale: they solve different jobs, health-tracker already exists and works, and keeping them separate gives the brand two distinct assets instead of one bloated tool. May merge into a "Terrain OS" later if it makes sense. Architecture decision filed for the SaaS spec.

#### A. Premium Substack
- **What:** Paid newsletter — deep-dive FDA regulatory analysis, macro-longevity economy reporting, personal protocol updates
- **Price:** $7-12/mo or $70-120/yr
- **Model archetype:** Mansi Hukmani's Chief Longevity Officer
- **Role in stack:** Top-of-funnel content engine → email list → conversion to all other offers
- **Build cost:** 1 long-form post / week (Dre's effort) + Substack platform ($)
- **Time to first revenue:** 30 days
- **Scalability:** High. Once subscribers, recurring.

#### B. Skool community (paid)
- **What:** Private paid community with monthly content drops, live Q&A, member protocols
- **Price:** $47-97/mo or $497/yr (founding member discount in year 1)
- **Model archetype:** Chris Duffin's "Endless Evolution" Skool
- **Role in stack:** Retention engine + recurring revenue + customer research (members tell you what to build next)
- **Build cost:** Skool platform ($99/mo) + 2-4 hrs/week community management
- **Compliance caveat:** MUST moderate per Section 2.3. If a member posts injection protocols, redirect to "talk to your doctor." Cannot host storefront integration.
- **Time to first revenue:** 14 days (setup) → 30 days (first paid members)
- **Scalability:** High. Mod-rate-per-member stays roughly constant until 1k+ members.

#### C. Reconstitution Calculator SaaS
- **What:** Mobile + web app solving the V_draw = Target Dose / C math for peptide reconstitution, with multi-vial tracking, BAC water volume calculator, syringe unit conversion, dosing logs, body composition tracking
- **Price:** Free tier (basic calc) + Pro subscription $9.99/mo or $79/yr
- **Model archetype:** Peptide Calculator / PepCalc (existing apps) — but with brand authority + Dre's UX polish
- **Role in stack:** Daily utility → retention → authority signal → leads to affiliate
- **Build cost:** Significant. Native iOS + Android + web. OR start with a web-only PWA (Progressive Web App) to validate, then go native.
- **MVP recommendation:** **Web-only PWA**, freemium, $9.99/mo Pro. 6-week build. Validates demand before native investment.
- **Compliance caveat:** Must explicitly state "informational and educational use only. Not medical advice. Consult your physician." in app copy, ToS, and onboarding.
- **Time to first revenue:** 60-90 days
- **Scalability:** Very high. Pure software. Can be white-labeled (see Stream M).

#### D. Flagship course — "The Dose of Proof Protocol"
- **What:** Self-paced video course on the recon framework: biomarker baseline → protocol selection → reconstitution math → outcome tracking → cycle management
- **Price:** $497 one-time or 3 × $197 payment plan
- **Format:** 8-12 modules, ~6-8 hours video, downloadable PDFs (reconstitution worksheet, biomarker tracking template), optional monthly cohort Q&A
- **Model archetype:** Jay Campbell's "Optimize Your Health with Therapeutic Peptides" + Duffin's protocol courses
- **Role in stack:** Mid-ticket core product. The "I bought something from Dose of Proof" anchor. Feeds 1:1 coaching backend.
- **Build cost:** High. ~80-120 hours of video production. Course platform (Teachable $39/mo or Kajabi $149/mo or Skool Classroom). Editing.
- **Time to first revenue:** 90-120 days (build) + 30 days to first cohort launch
- **Scalability:** Very high. Self-paced = no marginal cost per student.

#### E. Books + PDF guides
- **What:** Amazon KDP book (e.g., "Dose of Proof: A Citizen Scientist's Guide to Reclaiming Your Biology") + shorter PDF guides on specific topics (mitochondrial optimization, gut-brain axis, biomarker interpretation)
- **Price:** Book $14.99-24.99 / PDFs $19-47
- **Model archetype:** Jay Campbell's book library
- **Role in stack:** Long-tail SEO, authority signal, low-friction entry point (price point below $30 = impulse buy), gift-able
- **Build cost:** Medium. Book = ~40-80 hours. PDFs = ~5-10 hours each.
- **Time to first revenue:** 90 days (book) + 30 days (first PDF)
- **Scalability:** High. Royalties compound.

#### F. Podcast — "Dose of Proof"
- **See Section 8 for full architecture.**
- **Direct revenue:** Sponsorships (read ads, host-read) — $50-300 per 1k downloads per episode, depending on niche
- **Indirect revenue:** Every other offer benefits from podcast traffic
- **Time to first revenue:** 90-180 days (build audience → first sponsor)
- **Scalability:** High. Once cadence is set, marginal cost per episode is mostly time.

### Tier 2 — Partnership revenue (the fuel)

These streams require audience + trust but are the fastest path to revenue. The "as many streams as possible" mandate applies here.

#### G. High-ticket telehealth affiliations
- **Partners:** Marek Health (primary), Lifeforce (premium tier)
- **Commission:** Marek 4% / $20 per checkout; Lifeforce 16% recurring
- **Role in stack:** Convert "I want the peptides" intent into legitimate, supervised care. The "where do I get this legally?" answer.
- **Earnings model:** 100 Lifeforce customers × $1,500 annual subscription × 16% = $24,000/year recurring. Plus 200 Marek checkouts × $20 = $4,000 one-time.
- **Build cost:** Application + approval (2-4 weeks each). Content integration.
- **Compliance caveat:** These partners must remain in good standing with FDA. Any warning letter to them = immediate audit per Section 2.5.
- **Time to first revenue:** 30-60 days (post-approval)
- **Scalability:** High. Affiliate revenue scales with audience.

#### H. Foundational supplements affiliations
- **Partners:** Thorne (10-20% tiered), BiOptimizers (16-30%)
- **Role in stack:** Capture "I want to start somewhere basic" intent. Lower-ticket than telehealth.
- **Earnings model:** 500 supplement sales × $50 AOV × 20% = $5,000/month at scale
- **Build cost:** Application + content. Minimal.
- **Compliance caveat:** Thorne and BiOptimizers are well-established, low-risk. Monitor for any product-specific issues.
- **Time to first revenue:** 30 days
- **Scalability:** High.

#### I. Diagnostic platform affiliations
- **Partners:** InsideTracker (athletic focus, tiered)
- **Role in stack:** Capture "I want to baseline my biomarkers" intent. Educational alignment.
- **Earnings model:** 100 signups × $50 AOV × 20% = $1,000/month
- **Build cost:** Application + content.
- **Time to first revenue:** 30 days
- **Scalability:** Medium.

### Tier 3 — High-ticket backend (the high-margin)

The streams that require Dre's time but generate the highest revenue per customer. Capacity-limited by design.

#### J. 1:1 coaching / consulting
- **What:** 90-minute deep-dive protocol review + 3 months of async support. Dre personally.
- **Price:** $3,000-5,000 per engagement (limited to 4-6 per quarter to protect time)
- **Role in stack:** The "I want the actual person" tier. Backend of course / community.
- **Build cost:** Dre's time. Application form + scheduling + prep.
- **Capacity ceiling:** 24-30 clients per year max
- **Time to first revenue:** 14 days (form + scheduling)
- **Scalability:** Low by design. Time-bound.

#### K. Group mastermind
- **What:** Quarterly cohort of 12-20 members. Monthly 2-hour group call + private channel + annual in-person retreat (Dre's city, TBD).
- **Price:** $5,000-10,000/year per member
- **Role in stack:** Premium tier. The "I want peers + access" group.
- **Build cost:** Significant. Application + vetting + call cadence + retreat logistics.
- **Earnings model:** 15 members × $7,500/year = $112,500/year at scale
- **Time to first revenue:** 90 days (first cohort onboards)
- **Scalability:** Medium. Each cohort = linear effort, but tiered pricing helps.

#### L. Speaking + corporate workshops
- **What:** Keynote talks + half-day workshops for executive teams, founder retreats, longevity clinics
- **Price:** $10,000-25,000 per engagement
- **Role in stack:** Brand authority + high-margin revenue. Mostly inbound after authority is established.
- **Build cost:** Talk polish + travel + materials.
- **Earnings model:** 4-8 engagements/year × $15,000 avg = $60,000-120,000/year
- **Time to first revenue:** 180+ days (after authority established)
- **Scalability:** Low. Dre's time-bound.

### Tier 4 — Asset leverage (the optionality)

These streams unlock AFTER the core is built. They require audience + product-market-fit first.

#### M. SaaS white-label licensing
- **What:** License the Reconstitution Calculator to telehealth platforms (Marek, Lifeforce) for use in their patient portals
- **Price:** $5,000-20,000/year per partner + revenue share
- **Role in stack:** Turns the SaaS into a B2B asset. Multiplies the dev investment.
- **Build cost:** Zero (after SaaS is built)
- **Time to first revenue:** 180+ days (after SaaS has traction)
- **Scalability:** High. SaaS without per-user cost.

#### N. Sponsored content (owned channels only)
- **What:** Sponsored posts in Substack / podcast. NEVER in the public social feed (preserves feed integrity).
- **Price:** $5,000-15,000 per sponsored Substack post / podcast episode segment
- **Role in stack:** Premium inventory. Once owned channels are large, this is recurring.
- **Compliance caveat:** Sponsorship = clearly disclosed. "This episode is brought to you by..." No health claims for the sponsor in the post itself.
- **Time to first revenue:** 180+ days
- **Scalability:** High.

#### O. Course adaptations + partner co-marketing
- **What:** Custom course versions for telehealth platforms, supplement companies, diagnostic platforms. They pay to co-brand.
- **Price:** $25,000-100,000 per adaptation
- **Role in stack:** Course becomes a B2B product. One course → N adaptations.
- **Build cost:** Medium per adaptation.
- **Time to first revenue:** 180+ days
- **Scalability:** High. Each adaptation is leverage.

Authority compounds, virality is downstream.**

4. **1:1 coaching availability.** Open rolling (people apply any time) vs. quarterly cohort (4 per quarter, all start same month). **My pick: quarterly cohort. Easier to manage, creates natural urgency, allows cohort-based testimonials.**

5. **Mastermind launch timing.** Q4 2026 (post-podcast traction) vs. Q1 2027 (after the flywheel is established). **My pick: Q4 2026. Mastermind needs the podcast + course + community as upstream proof. By November, all three are live. The Q1 timing is safer but loses 3 months of compounding.**

6. **First paid product.** Course vs. Skool paid tier vs. SaaS Pro. Each has a different "first dollar" profile. **My pick: Substack paid + Skool paid (low friction, recurring) launch in parallel. Course is the bigger splash but takes 90 days to build. SaaS MVP is the highest-effort technical build.**

7. **Origin story detail level.** How much medical trauma to share publicly. This affects brand voice + audience trust + personal privacy. **My pick: detailed enough to be undeniable, anonymized where possible, with a clear "this is my story" framing. Not a self-help memoir — operational truth + the journey to function.**
