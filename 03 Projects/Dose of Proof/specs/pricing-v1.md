## 1. Pricing: Flagship Course — "The Dose of Proof Protocol"

### Inputs from /offers
- Business type: course (self-paced)
- Dream outcome: "I can read my own bloodwork, design a protocol, reconstitute and dose safely, and track my biomarkers over a 90-day cycle."
- Time to result: 90 days
- Effort: 4-6 hours/week
- Capacity: unlimited (self-paced)

### Floor analysis
- Fulfillment cost per buyer: $30 (course platform + video hosting + PDFs + email)
- Margin requirement: ≥30%
- Risk buffer: 5% refund assumption = $25
- **Floor: $55.** Selling at $497 = 89% margin. Massive headroom — the price isn't constrained by cost.

### Anchor analysis
| Anchor type | Reference | Strength |
|-------------|-----------|----------|
| **Alternative cost** | Functional medicine consults: $300-500/hr × 3-5 visits = $900-2,500 | **STRONG** — verifiable, real, higher than our price |
| Future cost of inaction | "vs. mis-dosing and ending up in the ER" | Moderate — emotionally strong but harder to quantify |
| Total value | $497 course + $300 in bonuses = $797 value | Weak — bonus inflation theater |

**Anchor chosen: Alternative cost.**
**Framing:** "Most people pay $1,500-2,500 for 3-5 functional medicine consults that don't include the recon math or the 90-day protocol framework. The Dose of Proof Protocol delivers the framework + the math + the protocol for $497."

### Price + structure
- **Lump-sum: $497** (charm pricing at this level is OK; $500 also works but $497 reads as a deal at the boundary)
- **3-pay: 3 × $185 = $555** (multi-pay is 11.7% MORE than lump-sum — 10.4% discount for full pay)
- **Premium for full pay: 10.4%** ($58 saved by paying in full)

**Why 3-pay, not 6-pay:** the course is delivered in 90 days. 3 monthly installments maps to the delivery arc. 6-pay would create payment-without-engagement (per anti-patterns).

### Anti-pattern gate
- [x] Anchor is real (functional medicine consults are verifiable)
- [x] No fake prior price (no "was $997, now $497")
- [x] Charm pricing appropriate at this level ($497 reads correctly)
- [x] No decoy tier (single-tier course is the right shape)
- [x] No "contact us" pricing
- [x] Multi-pay has full-pay premium (10.4%)
- [x] Price has clear payment structure
- [x] Price change from $497/3×$197 → $497/3×$185 is documented

## 4. Pricing: 1:1 Coaching — "Protocol Review with Dre" ⚠

### Inputs from /offers
- Business type: 1:1 coaching (time-bound)
- Dream outcome: "Dre personally reviewed my bloodwork, walked me through my recon plan, I have 3 months async support."
- Time to result: 30 days
- Effort: 90-min call + 30 min/week async
- Capacity: 4-6/quarter (Dre's time)

### Floor analysis — **CRITICAL FINDING**
- Fulfillment: 90-min call + 3 months async = ~10 hours of Dre's time
- Dre's time at $300/hr floor (functional medicine consult rate): $3,000
- Platform + admin: $200
- Refund risk: 10% (premium 1:1 has higher refund rate due to "not what I expected")
- **Floor: $3,500**

**⚠ The original offers spec had $3,500. The pricing analysis confirms this is the floor — not the recommended price.**

### Why $5,000, not $3,500
At $3,500, the offer is 1.0× margin — meaning any operational friction (no-show, refund, prep time overrun) eats the margin to zero or negative. For a 1:1 offer where Dre's time is the deliverable, the price must price Dre's time at a coaching premium (2-3× his hourly rate), not a consulting rate.

- Dre's time at coaching rate (2× hourly = $600/hr × 10 hrs): $6,000
- Recommended price: **$5,000** (slight discount from full coaching rate because of the async component)
- This prices the engagement at $500/hr effective — premium but defensible

### Anchor analysis
| Anchor type | Reference | Strength |
|-------------|-----------|----------|
| **Future cost of inaction** | "vs. 6 more months of mis-protocoling, mis-dosing, or guessing" | **STRONG** — emotional + specific |
| Alternative cost | Functional medicine practitioner: $300-500/hr × 4-6 visits = $1,200-3,000 (no async follow-up) | Moderate |
| Total value | $5,000 = 10 hrs Dre time + 3 mo async = $X/hr value | Weak |

**Anchor chosen: Future cost of inaction.**
**Framing:** "Six more months of guessing costs you more in mis-doses, ER scares, and stalled biomarkers than $5,000. This is a 90-minute deep-dive + 3 months of async adjustment with the person who built the framework. 6 spots per quarter. No payment plan."

### Price + structure
- **$5,000 per engagement** (one-time, no multi-pay)
- **No payment plan** — anchors premium + filters for commitment + eliminates refund-without-engagement pattern
- 6 clients per year max (1.5/quarter)
- Application + vetting call required

**Why no payment plan on 1:1:** per /pricing anti-patterns, multi-pay on high-ticket 1:1 creates payment-without-engagement risk. The buyer is paying $X/month but Dre's time is committed up front. A $5k lump-sum filters for buyers who are serious.

### Anti-pattern gate
- [x] Anchor is real (months of bad protocols has a real cost)
- [x] No fake prior price
- [x] Premium pricing at this level ($5,000 reads correctly, NOT $4,997)
- [x] No decoy tier (single tier is right for 1:1)
- [x] "Contact us" replaced with application form (still an anchor)
- [x] No multi-pay (no premium-for-full-pay needed)
- [x] Price has clear structure
- [x] Price change from $3,500 → $5,000 is documented and justified

**Gate: PASS (with recommendation to update offers spec).**

| 6 | **Flagship course** | **$497** | Course-ready buyer | Mid-ticket core product |
| 7 | 1:1 coaching | $5,000 | Stuck + serious | High-ticket backend |
| 8 | Mastermind | $5,000-7,500/yr | Operator + committed | Premium cohort |

**Multiplier check:** each step is 2-5× the previous. No awkward gaps.

### Decoy design (the 3-tier pattern across the stack)

When a buyer is considering the flagship course at $497, they're also seeing:
- Skool at $47-67/mo (cheaper per month but recurring)
- 1:1 at $5,000 (10× more expensive but personalized)

The $5,000 1:1 makes $497 course look like a steal. The Skool at $47-67/mo looks like ongoing access vs. one-time. The 1:1 is the decoy that anchors the course.

When a buyer is considering the mastermind at $7,500, they're also seeing:
- 1:1 at $5,000 (cheaper, but solo)
- Skool at $670/yr (much cheaper, but no Dre access)

The 1:1 is the alternative-cost anchor. The Skool is the volume anchor. The mastermind sits above both with a different value prop (peer group + retreat).

## 7. Findings (the things the user should know)

### Finding 1 — 1:1 coaching under-priced in offers spec ⚠
The offers spec had $3,500. The /pricing analysis shows this is the floor, not the recommended price. At $3,500, the offer has 0% margin. **Recommendation: bump to $5,000.** This is the only pricing change from the offers spec.

**Reasoning:** at $3,500, Dre's effective hourly rate is $350 (for 10 hours of work) — that's the same as a functional medicine consultant who doesn't have a brand, an audience, or async follow-up. The premium 1:1 should price Dre's brand + async component at 2× the consultant rate.

### Finding 2 — 3-pay math was backwards in offers spec
The offers spec had "3 × $197 = $591" (multi-pay is 19% MORE than lump-sum). Per /pricing, multi-pay should be 5-15% MORE, not 19%. **Fixed to 3 × $185 = $555** (multi-pay is 11.7% more, lump-sum is 10.4% discount).

### Finding 3 — Skool $670/yr is right at the annual norm
$670/yr with monthly at $67 = 16.7% annual discount = 2 months free. This is the standard SaaS annual discount. No change needed, but the "why" is documented here.

### Finding 4 — Mastermind is the highest-margin offer
At $7,500/yr × 15 members = $112,500 cohort revenue vs. ~$32,000 cost. 71% margin. This is the cashflow engine for Q4 2026. Capacity is the constraint, not margin.

### Finding 5 — Course at $497 is the volume driver
At founding-cohort target of 100 students × $497 = $49,700. Plus evergreen at 30/mo = $14,910/mo recurring-ish. The course is the cash engine for Q3.

### Finding 6 — Skool Inner Circle is the retention moat
$67/mo × 150 members = $10,050/mo. Once members are in, churn is the only risk. Moderation quality = retention. This is the recurring base for the business.

### Finding 7 — SaaS Pro is the most defensible (long-term)
Pure software, scales without Dre's time. The 12-month compounding is the long play. The MVP validates demand; the long-term is white-label licensing (Stream M in the offers spec).
