## 0. Voice Calibration — Dre's Voice

### The 7 universal voice rules (applied to Dre)

| # | Rule | Dre's expression |
|---|------|------------------|
| 1 | Specific > generic | "I had C1-C2 craniocervical instability" not "I had neck issues" |
| 2 | Short + medium sentences | Mix of 6-12 word punches with 18-22 word explanations |
| 3 | Active voice dominant | "I built my own tracking system" not "a tracking system was built" |
| 4 | No filler | Cut every "in today's world", "leverage", "going forward" |
| 5 | Operator > brand voice | Dre speaking, not "Dose of Proof™" speaking |
| 6 | Builder not marketer | "Here's how it works" not "Unlock the power" |
| 7 | Honest about limitations | "I'm still in this process myself" — Dre is the first case study |

### Dre-specific voice (encoded from the brand-voice file)

**Tone:** First-person, contrarian, evidence-anchored, builder-proud, raw-not-performative.

**Vocabulary anchors (from Dre's own writing):**
- "Citizen scientist" / "raw stories, real data, protocols"
- "Symptom Whack-a-Mole" (his coinage for fragmented symptom-treating)
- "Terrain" / "the full terrain" (mold + minerals + nervous system + connective tissue)
- "Vagus nerve" / "mast cell activation" / "MCAS" / "hEDS" / "CCI" (the technical language, used precisely)
- "Upstream work" vs "managing symptoms forever"
- "Map the real terrain" / "show me the data"
- "I'm still in it" (the honesty anchor)
- "Gaslit by the system" / "you're not broken and you're not alone"
- "Craniocervical" / "Blair upper cervical" / "TyTron infrared scans" (the clinical specificity)

**Banned vocabulary (compliance + voice):**
- "Cure" / "treat" / "heal" (compliance — see business-model-v1.md Section 2.2)
- "Prescribe" / "diagnose" (compliance — operator is not a physician)
- "Secret" / "they don't want you to know" (banned hype)
- "Game-changing" / "revolutionary" / "breakthrough" (banned hype)
- "Unleash" / "unlock" (banned)
- "Synergy" / "leverage" / "value-add" (corporate sludge)
- "Fake scarcity" / "limited spots" (Dre explicitly rejects in Email 15)
- "Comforting" / "we're here for you" (Dre is direct, not soft)

**Sentence rhythm:** Dre writes in short declarative sentences mixed with longer explanatory ones. Reads like a smart friend who's been through hell and is telling you what they learned. No corporate hedge, no inspirational poster.

**Tone shifts by surface:**

| Surface | Tone | Length |
|---------|------|--------|
| Sales page | Confident, mechanism-forward, raw | 2,000-2,500 words |
| Email | Conversational, 1:1 letter voice, honest | 200-500 words |
| Landing page | Direct, single-message | 300-600 words |
| Ad copy | Punchy, outcome-first | 10-30 words |
| Sales call | Listening-heavy, story-driven | n/a (live) |

**The voice test:** would I say this sentence to a smart friend who has been through the same hell? If yes, ship. If no, rewrite.
