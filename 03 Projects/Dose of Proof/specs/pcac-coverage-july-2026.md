## Phase 3 — Telehealth Pivot (August 2026)

**Goal:** Convert the post-PCAC regulatory clarity + the launch sales momentum into telehealth affiliate revenue. This is the high-ticket backend becoming real.

### The positioning

After the FDA PCAC decision, the audience will ask: "Okay, so how do I get [compound X] legally?"

The brand's answer (compliant + specific):
> "If you're running a protocol that requires [compound], the legal pathway is supervised care through a 503A compounding pharmacy or a telehealth partner with board-certified prescribers.
>
> I'm not a doctor. I don't prescribe. I teach the framework. For protocol decisions, work with your physician or a vetted telehealth partner.
>
> The Dose of Proof Protocol course teaches you to bring the data to your doctor — not to self-prescribe."

### Compliant telehealth handoff language (the canonical phrasing)

> "I work with telehealth partners who operate within 503A compounding pharmacy rules and use board-certified prescribers. These are not gray-market sourcing — they're licensed, supervised, and legally compliant.
>
> My affiliate partnerships are with platforms that:
> - Use board-certified physicians
> - Operate through 503A compounding pharmacies
> - Require biomarker baselines before prescribing
> - Comply with FTC substantiation standards
>
> I'm still in this process myself. I work with my own physician. I recommend you do the same.
>
> [Specific telehealth partner — Marek Health or Lifeforce]: [affiliate link]"

### Compliant telehealth handoff (the DO NOT list)

- ❌ "I recommend [telehealth partner]" without specifying the supervised-care angle
- ❌ "Use my code for X% off" (without the supervised-care context)
- ❌ Affiliate link without the 5 unbreakable rules context
- ❌ Any implication that the partner is "the only" or "the best" — frame as one vetted option
