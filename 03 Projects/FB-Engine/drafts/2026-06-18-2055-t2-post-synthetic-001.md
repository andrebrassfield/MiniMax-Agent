Yeah, this group is for local service business owners who want to share proof-based strategies for growing their business without paid ads — that's a real problem in a lot of operator circles.

The thing I'd add: Average missed HVAC call costs $400-600 in lost revenue. At 8 missed calls/day (conservative for a 1-truck shop), annualized burn is $1.0M-$1.5M. The math: 8 calls × $500/job × 320 working days/year. *(Source: HVAC.com + ServiceTitan customer data 2024)*

In our experience, the actual bottleneck is the missed-call math, not the labor cost — not the thing most folks focus on. The per-event cost in the missed-call math, not the labor cost is what compounds — the hourly/dollar savings people chase is the wrong unit.
