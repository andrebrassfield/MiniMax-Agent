# Email Sequence Blueprints — 7 Templates

Email is where most buyer relationships live. Each sequence has a specific job and a specific cadence. Use these blueprints to draft, then customize for your voice and offer.

---

## 1. Welcome Sequence (new subscriber)

**Job:** Introduce yourself, deliver on the opt-in promise, build trust, set up future emails.

**Length:** 5–7 emails.
**Cadence:** Email 1 immediately. Email 2 next day. Then every 2–3 days.

### Email 1 — Deliver the promise (immediate)

**Subject:** Welcome to [Brand] — your [opt-in] is inside
**Goal:** Deliver the lead magnet. Set expectations.

**Anatomy:**
- "Here's your [opt-in]" (deliver the thing)
- 1–2 sentences on what's coming next
- P.S. with the next step (reply, share, etc.)

**Length:** 100–200 words.

### Email 2 — Origin story (Day 1)

**Subject:** Why I built [Brand] / How I got here
**Goal:** Build personal connection.

**Anatomy:**
- Your origin story (specific moment that started this)
- Why you built the offer (the buyer's problem, your solution)
- Bridge to the next email

**Length:** 200–400 words.

### Email 3 — The mistake most people make (Day 3)

**Subject:** The biggest mistake I see [audience] making
**Goal:** Establish expertise + plant the problem.

**Anatomy:**
- The common mistake (specific, not generic)
- Why it fails (with example)
- Brief mention of the alternative (sets up your offer without selling)

**Length:** 200–400 words.

### Email 4 — Case study / proof (Day 5)

**Subject:** How [client/customer] did [outcome]
**Goal:** Show that the method works.

**Anatomy:**
- Buyer profile
- What they tried before
- What they did (your method)
- Result (specific)

**Length:** 300–500 words.

### Email 5 — The mechanism (Day 8)

**Subject:** The [method name] — how it actually works
**Goal:** Teach the mechanism. Builds Perceived Likelihood.

**Anatomy:**
- The mechanism (named, explained)
- Why it's different
- Soft CTA to learn more

**Length:** 300–500 words.

### Email 6 — Objection handling (Day 11)

**Subject:** "But what if [objection]?"
**Goal:** Handle the most common objection.

**Anatomy:**
- Acknowledge the objection (in their words)
- Address it directly
- Bridge to the next email

**Length:** 200–400 words.

### Email 7 — Soft pitch (Day 14)

**Subject:** If you're ready to [outcome], here's how
**Goal:** First explicit pitch for the offer.

**Anatomy:**
- Restate the outcome
- Restate the offer (link to sales page)
- Guarantee restated
- CTA

**Length:** 200–400 words.

## 2. Pre-Launch Sequence (waitlist)

**Job:** Build anticipation before cart opens. Seed the problem. Tease the solution.

**Length:** 3–5 emails.
**Cadence:** Daily in the final week before launch.

### Email 1 — "Something is coming" (T-7 days)

**Subject:** I've been working on something
**Goal:** Tease the launch.

**Anatomy:**
- "I've been building something for [audience]"
- Don't reveal the offer yet
- Ask them to reply if interested

**Length:** 100–200 words.

### Email 2 — The problem (T-5 days)

**Subject:** The [problem] most [audience] face
**Goal:** Plant the problem deeply.

**Anatomy:**
- The problem, in detail
- Why it's not getting solved by what they've tried
- Bridge to "what if there was a different way"

**Length:** 300–500 words.

### Email 3 — The mechanism (T-3 days)

**Subject:** The [method name] — what it is
**Goal:** Reveal the mechanism behind the upcoming offer.

**Anatomy:**
- The mechanism, named and explained
- Why it works
- "If you want the full thing, [cart opens T-2]"

**Length:** 300–500 words.

### Email 4 — Cart opens tomorrow (T-1 day)

**Subject:** Doors open tomorrow at [time]
**Goal:** Drive anticipation to specific time.

**Anatomy:**
- "Tomorrow at [time], cart opens"
- Brief reminder of the offer
- "Set a reminder" / "Reply 'in' if you're in"

**Length:** 100–250 words.

### Email 5 — Cart opens NOW (T-0)

**Subject:** Cart is open — [offer name]
**Goal:** Drive to sales page immediately.

**Anatomy:**
- Cart is open
- Link to sales page
- Brief offer reminder
- First scarcity mention if applicable

**Length:** 200–400 words.

## 3. Open Cart Sequence (launch day 1)

**Job:** Drive immediate action. Tap the anticipation.

**Length:** 1–3 emails.
**Cadence:** Email 1 at launch. Email 2 next morning. Email 3 (optional) next afternoon.

### Email 1 — Cart is open (T-0)

See Pre-Launch Email 5 above.

### Email 2 — Why I built this for you (T-1 day morning)

**Subject:** Why this offer exists
**Goal:** Deepen emotional investment.

**Anatomy:**
- The personal why
- Who it's for specifically
- "If that's you, here's the link"

**Length:** 300–500 words.

### Email 3 — Quick case study (T-1 day afternoon)

**Subject:** Quick story: how [client] [outcome]
**Goal:** Add proof at the moment of decision.

**Anatomy:**
- 1-paragraph case study
- Link to sales page

**Length:** 150–300 words.

---

## 4. Mid-Launch Sequence (days 2–5)

**Job:** Handle objections, add proof, keep momentum.

**Length:** 2–3 emails.
**Cadence:** Every other day.

### Email 1 — Most common objection (Day 2)

**Subject:** "But what if [objection]?"
**Goal:** Address the main blocker.

**Anatomy:**
- Acknowledge the objection
- Address it
- Re-link to sales page

**Length:** 200–400 words.

### Email 2 — Two more case studies (Day 4)

**Subject:** Two more quick stories
**Goal:** Add proof.

**Anatomy:**
- 2 short case studies with specific numbers
- Link to sales page

**Length:** 200–400 words.

### Email 3 — FAQ (Day 5, optional)

**Subject:** Your top 5 questions, answered
**Goal:** Handle remaining objections.

**Anatomy:**
- 5 FAQ items, each 2–3 sentences
- Link to sales page

**Length:** 400–600 words.

---

## 5. Close Cart Sequence (final days)

**Job:** Drive urgency (real, not manufactured). Last call.

**Length:** 2–3 emails.
**Cadence:** Daily.

### Email 1 — 48 hours left (T-2)

**Subject:** 48 hours left to join [offer]
**Goal:** Drive decision.

**Anatomy:**
- Time-bound reminder
- Restate offer
- Link to sales page

**Length:** 200–400 words.

### Email 2 — 24 hours left (T-1)

**Subject:** Last day / doors close tomorrow
**Goal:** Final push.

**Anatomy:**
- "Tomorrow at [time], doors close"
- Who the offer is for (and who it isn't)
- Link

**Length:** 200–400 words.

### Email 3 — Doors closing tonight (T-0)

**Subject:** Doors close in [X] hours
**Goal:** Last call.

**Anatomy:**
- Specific closing time
- "If you've been on the fence, this is it"
- Final link

**Length:** 100–250 words.

## 6. Abandoned Cart Sequence

**Job:** Recover the buyer who started checkout but didn't complete.

**Length:** 3 emails.
**Cadence:** 1 hour after abandonment, 1 day, 3 days.

### Email 1 — You left something in your cart (1 hour)

**Subject:** You left [offer] in your cart
**Goal:** Remind + recover.

**Anatomy:**
- "You started checking out for [offer]"
- Quick reminder of what's included
- "Click here to finish"
- Optional: small incentive (only if you have one — don't manufacture)

**Length:** 100–250 words.

### Email 2 — Answering common questions (1 day)

**Subject:** A few questions about [offer]
**Goal:** Handle objections that caused abandonment.

**Anatomy:**
- 3–4 FAQs
- Restate the guarantee
- Link to checkout

**Length:** 300–500 words.

### Email 3 — Last reminder (3 days)

**Subject:** Your [offer] is still waiting
**Goal:** Final attempt.

**Anatomy:**
- Brief reminder
- Restate guarantee
- "If now isn't the time, I get it — but here's the link if you change your mind"

**Length:** 100–250 words.

## 7. Post-Purchase Sequence

**Job:** Onboard the buyer. Set expectations. Maximize success (which maximizes retention and referrals).

**Length:** 3–5 emails over 30 days.
**Cadence:** Immediately, Day 2, Day 7, Day 14, Day 30.

### Email 1 — Welcome + first step (immediate)

**Subject:** You're in. Here's what to do first.
**Goal:** Onboard. Reduce "now what?" confusion.

**Anatomy:**
- "Welcome to [offer]"
- The single most important first action
- How to get support

**Length:** 200–400 words.

### Email 2 — What to expect (Day 2)

**Subject:** What the next [X days/weeks] looks like
**Goal:** Set expectations. Reduce anxiety.

**Anatomy:**
- Timeline of what happens
- What they need to do
- What you'll do

**Length:** 300–500 words.

### Email 3 — Quick win (Day 7)

**Subject:** Your first [win/milestone]
**Goal:** Drive engagement.

**Anatomy:**
- Specific action they can take this week
- Expected outcome
- "Reply and tell me how it went"

**Length:** 200–400 words.

### Email 4 — Mid-program check-in (Day 14)

**Subject:** Quick check-in
**Goal:** Catch disengagement early.

**Anatomy:**
- "How's it going?"
- Common blockers at this stage
- "Reply if you need anything"

**Length:** 100–250 words.

### Email 5 — Final push + referral ask (Day 30)

**Subject:** You're almost there
**Goal:** Drive to completion + referrals.

**Anatomy:**
- "X days left in the program"
- Final actions to take
- "If you know someone who'd benefit, here's a referral link"

**Length:** 200–400 words.

## Email sequencing anti-patterns

### Too many emails
15+ emails in a "welcome sequence" burns the list. 5–7 is enough.

### Too frequent
Emailing daily to a welcome sequence tanks open rates. Every 2–3 days is the floor.

### No clear next step
Email that doesn't ask for an action (reply, click, etc.) is filler.

### Repeating the same email
"Welcome to [brand]" twice in the first week because two systems sent it. Dedup.

### Email without specific subject line
Subject lines that don't earn the open kill the sequence. Test every subject line.

### Sequence that doesn't match the offer
Welcome sequence about pricing theory for a course on email marketing. Match the sequence to the offer's actual topic.

### Sending to unengaged subscribers
Subscribers who haven't opened in 90 days shouldn't get the welcome sequence — they should get a re-engagement sequence (or be cleaned).

## Quick checklist per email

Before any email ships:

- [ ] Specific subject line (3+ variants tested)
- [ ] Pre-header that doesn't repeat subject
- [ ] One clear CTA
- [ ] First-person CTA button
- [ ] No banned hype words
- [ ] Voice matches the brand
- [ ] Mobile-friendly (single column, <600px wide, large CTA)
- [ ] Plain text or text-only version (some clients block images)
- [ ] Unsubscribe link visible (CAN-SPAM compliance)
