# Launch Anti-Patterns Checklist

The full banned-moves list for launches. A launch that ships with any of these is weaker, not stronger. Run this list before the launch goes live.

---

## 1. Fake evergreen countdown

**What it looks like:** "5 hours left!" on an offer that's always available. Countdown that resets on every visit.

**Why it's banned:** Buyers can tell. Manufactured countdown kills urgency for real countdowns.

**Replacement:** Real cohort cap. Real cart-close date. Real scarcity.

---

## 2. "Limited spots" without an actual cap

**What it looks like:** "Only 5 spots left!" with no actual cap. Operator keeps adding past the stated limit.

**Why it's banned:** Once buyers find out, the cap loses all force. Future launches suffer.

**Replacement:** Real cohort cap. Enforced. Stop selling when cap is hit.

---

## 3. Cart-close extension

**What it looks like:** "Doors close Friday" → "Actually, doors close Sunday." Once is forgivable. Twice destroys trust.

**Why it's banned:** Each extension trains buyers to wait. Future cart-close dates lose force.

**Replacement:** Lock the cart-close date. Don't move it.

**Exception:** technical issues with the sales page or checkout. Move once, communicate clearly, never again.

---

## 4. Reopening "one more spot"

**What it looks like:** "One more buyer, then we're really closing." Then another "one more spot."

**Why it's banned:** Each reopen trains buyers that closure isn't real. The cap is theater.

**Replacement:** When the cohort fills, switch to waitlist for the next cohort.

---

## 5. Launch to unengaged list

**What it looks like:** Sending the launch sequence to subscribers who haven't opened in 6+ months.

**Why it's banned:** Burns sender reputation. Future emails to engaged subscribers go to spam.

**Replacement:** Segment the list. Send launch to engaged subscribers (opened 3+ in last 30 days). Suppress unengaged or send re-engagement sequence separately.

---

## 6. Stacked fakes

**What it looks like:** Fake countdown + fake scarcity + fake bonus values + fake testimonials, all on the same launch.

**Why it's banned:** Buyers see through it. The launch reads as a scam.

**Replacement:** Real urgency, real scarcity, real proof. Pick one fake (if any) and make it real.

---

## 7. No cart-close plan

**What it looks like:** Cart opens, sales come in, no plan for closing. Cart stays open indefinitely.

**Why it's banned:** Without a real close, the launch becomes evergreen-by-default. Lower conversion than a real launch.

**Replacement:** Lock cart-close date. Build the calendar backwards from it.

---

## 8. Overselling

**What it looks like:** Selling 100 cohort spots with capacity for 30.

**Why it's banned:** Fulfillment breaks. Buyer experience tanks. Refund disputes.

**Replacement:** Match sales to capacity. If you oversell, refund the excess.

---

## 9. Cart-close re-engagement to non-buyers

**What it looks like:** Sending "the cohort closed, here's the waitlist" email without a real waitlist plan.

**Why it's banned:** Burns the launch list's value without capturing it.

**Replacement:** Real waitlist for the next cohort. Real early-bird pricing for waitlist members.

---

## 10. Launch without social

**What it looks like:** Email-only launch. No social posts during pre-launch or cart-open.

**Why it's banned:** Email-only launches miss the audience that's on social but not email.

**Replacement:** Email + social. Match cadence.

---

## 11. Launch without paid amplification

**What it looks like:** Organic-only launch. No budget for ads or promotion.

**Why it's banned:** Misses the audience that's not on email or social (cold traffic).

**Replacement:** Even a small ad budget ($500–$2,000) lifts a launch. Allocate for cold traffic.

---

## 12. Same email sent twice

**What it looks like:** Two systems send the "cart is open" email to the same subscriber.

**Why it's banned:** Looks unprofessional. Buyer marks as spam.

**Replacement:** Dedup systems before launch. Test the welcome flow with a new email.

---

## 13. Cart-open without sales page

**What it looks like:** Cart opens, buyer clicks the link, sees a 404 or "coming soon" page.

**Why it's banned:** Cart-open anticipation dies instantly. Buyer leaves.

**Replacement:** Test the sales page + checkout the day before cart opens. Multiple devices.

---

## 14. Email without link

**What it looks like:** "Cart is open!" but the email doesn't link to the sales page.

**Why it's banned:** Friction. Buyer has to find the page themselves.

**Replacement:** Every launch email has a clear link to the sales page.

---

## 15. Continuing to sell after cart-close

**What it looks like:** Sending launch emails to non-buyers after the cart has closed.

**Why it's banned:** Buyer didn't buy when it was open. They won't buy now. Burns trust.

**Replacement:** Switch to waitlist + post-purchase at cart-close. Stop selling.

---

## 16. Multiple close-cart emails in one day

**What it looks like:** "Final hours!" at 9am, 1pm, 5pm. Three emails in 8 hours.

**Why it's banned:** Buyer unsubscribes. Saturated inbox.

**Replacement:** One email per day during close-cart. Maximum 3 total across the close-cart phase.

---

## 17. Manufactured urgency layered on real urgency

**What it looks like:** Real cohort cap + fake countdown timer + "limited time" with no deadline + "only X spots" without cap.

**Why it's banned:** Too many constraints compete for attention. Buyers get distrustful.

**Replacement:** One primary urgency. Real cohort cap OR real cart-close date, not both faked together.

---

## 18. Empty scarcity

**What it looks like:** "3 seats left!" when there are 8. Falsifying scarcity.

**Why it's banned:** Buyers can do the math (or ask). Once they catch it, all scarcity claims collapse.

**Replacement:** Real cohort cap, enforced.

---

## 19. Pre-launch without value

**What it looks like:** 5 pre-launch emails that are 80% "buy buy buy" with 20% content.

**Why it's banned:** Pre-launch should teach. Pure hype emails train buyers to ignore.

**Replacement:** 80% value, 20% anticipation-building.

---

## 20. Launching without fulfillment plan

**What it looks like:** Cart opens, sales come in, no plan for delivering the offer.

**Why it's banned:** Buyer pays, gets nothing (or gets a half-baked experience). Refunds, chargebacks, bad reviews.

**Replacement:** Fulfillment plan before launch. Onboarding emails queued. Support bandwidth verified.

---

## Quick checklist (use before launch goes live)

Before cart opens, every item must be NO:

- [ ] No fake evergreen countdown
- [ ] No "limited spots" without an actual cap
- [ ] No planned cart-close extension
- [ ] No planned reopening after close
- [ ] No launch to unengaged list
- [ ] No stacked fakes (countdown + scarcity + bonuses + testimonials)
- [ ] No missing cart-close plan
- [ ] No overselling vs. fulfillment capacity
- [ ] No cart-close re-engagement without waitlist plan
- [ ] No launch without social
- [ ] No launch without paid amplification (optional but recommended)
- [ ] No duplicate emails
- [ ] No cart-open without sales page tested
- [ ] No email without link to sales page
- [ ] No selling after cart-close
- [ ] No multiple close-cart emails in one day
- [ ] No manufactured urgency layered on real urgency
- [ ] No empty scarcity
- [ ] No pre-launch without value
- [ ] No launching without fulfillment plan

If any item is YES, fix before cart opens. Launch anti-patterns are visible to buyers immediately and hard to recover from.
