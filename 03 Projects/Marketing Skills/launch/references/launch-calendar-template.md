# Launch Calendar Templates — Day-by-Day for Each Launch Type

A locked launch calendar is the difference between a launch that ships and one that drifts. Build the calendar backwards from cart-close. Lock dates. Don't move them.

## Evergreen Launch Calendar

Evergreen launches don't have a calendar — they're always on. The "calendar" is the trigger-based email sequence.

| Trigger | Email | Subject | Delay |
|---------|-------|---------|-------|
| New subscriber | Email 1 | "Welcome + deliver lead magnet" | Immediate |
| After lead magnet | Email 2 | "Origin story" | 1 day |
| After origin | Email 3 | "Common mistake" | 2 days |
| After mistake | Email 4 | "Mechanism reveal" | 3 days |
| After mechanism | Email 5 | "Case study" | 3 days |
| After case study | Email 6 | "FAQ" | 3 days |
| After FAQ | Email 7 | "Soft pitch" | 3 days |
| Cart abandon | Email 8 | "You left [offer] in your cart" | 1 hour |
| After cart abandon | Email 9 | "Common questions" | 1 day |
| After cart abandon | Email 10 | "Last reminder" | 3 days |

## Calendar-building rules

### Lock dates before building emails

Pick the cart-open date first. Pick the cart-close date second. Build the calendar backwards from there. Don't pick dates based on when the copy is done.

### Don't move dates mid-launch

Once cart-open and cart-close are locked, they don't move. Moving dates erodes trust.

**Exception:** technical issues with the sales page or checkout. Move once, communicate clearly.

### Match email count to launch length

| Launch length | Email count (to launch list) |
|---------------|------------------------------|
| 5 days | 8–10 |
| 7 days | 10–14 |
| 10 days | 14–18 |
| 14 days | 16–20 |

More emails than that = burnout. Fewer = launch feels sparse.

### Match social to email

Every email day should have at least one social post (cart-open day, multiple). Days without emails can still have social posts.

### One cart-open. One cart-close.

Multiple cart-opens or cart-closes train buyers that deadlines aren't real. Pick one of each.

---

## Calendar anti-patterns

### Calendar built forwards from "now"
Build backwards from cart-close. Forward-built calendars drift as the launch progresses.

### Calendar without specific dates
"Soon" and "next week" don't anchor urgency. Specific dates do.

### Calendar that changes weekly
"This Friday... actually next Friday... actually Friday after that." Each move destroys the next deadline.

### Calendar with too many emails
30 emails in a 7-day launch = burnout. Stay in the 8–20 range.

### Calendar with too few emails
3 emails in a 14-day launch = launch feels absent. Stay in the 8–20 range.

### Calendar without post-launch plan
Cart closes and... silence. Always plan post-launch (waitlist + post-purchase + re-engagement).

### Calendar without social
Email-only launches miss social audiences. Run both.

---

## Quick checklist

Before locking the calendar:

- [ ] Cart-open date set (specific date + time + timezone)
- [ ] Cart-close date set (specific date + time + timezone)
- [ ] Launch type chosen (PLF, audience, signature, evergreen)
- [ ] Calendar built backwards from cart-close
- [ ] Email count matches launch length
- [ ] Social posts match email cadence
- [ ] Post-launch plan in calendar (waitlist + post-purchase)
- [ ] Calendar locked (dates don't move)
- [ ] All stakeholders aligned on dates
- [ ] Backup plan for technical issues
