# Open Cart Phase — Cart Opens, Buyers Start Arriving

The cart-open phase is where pre-launch anticipation converts to revenue. Done well, you capture most of the launch's revenue in the first 24–48 hours. Done poorly, the launch drags and the close-cart phase has to do too much work.

**Length:** 1–3 days.
**Goal:** Convert the warm audience fast. Tap the anticipation.

---

## Open cart principles

### Front-load the conversion

The buyers most likely to convert are the ones who joined the waitlist, opened every pre-launch email, and have been primed for weeks. They convert on day 1–2.

**Implication:** the cart-open emails should be optimized for warm buyers, not cold ones. The cold buyers need more setup; the warm buyers need an action prompt.

### Tap anticipation immediately

When the cart opens, send fast. Email at the exact minute the cart opens. Don't make the warm buyer wait for the morning batch.

### Multiple touchpoints in 24 hours

Cart-open day should have 2–3 emails:
- The "cart is open" email at cart-open time.
- A "why I built this" email in the evening.
- A "quick case study" email the next morning.

The buyer who missed the first email catches the second. The buyer who needs one more nudge gets the third.

---

## Open cart sequence (default 3-email / 24 hours)

### Email 1 — Cart is open (T-0, cart-open minute)

**Subject:** "[Offer name] is open — [outcome]"

**Body anatomy:**
- "Cart is open. [Offer name] is live."
- Brief reminder of the offer (link to sales page)
- First scarcity mention if applicable (cohort cap, calendar window)
- Time-bound reminder of cart-close

**Length:** 200–400 words.

**CTA:** Link to sales page.

**Sample:**

> Cart is open.
>
> The 90-Day MRR Sprint is live. 12-week coaching engagement. Written pricing audit by Day 14. 30-day outbound plan by Day 30. 90-day conditional guarantee.
>
> Most founders pay $25,000+ for a sales consultant who takes 6 months to ramp. We do this in 90 days for $7,500.
>
> Cohort cap: 8 founders. Next cohort opens Q1 2027.
>
> [YES, I WANT THE SPRINT — $7,500]
>
> Doors close Friday at 5pm ET.

---

### Email 2 — Why I built this (T-0, evening)

**Subject:** "Why I built the 90-Day MRR Sprint"

**Body anatomy:**
- The personal why (specific moment that started this)
- Who the offer is for (and who it isn't)
- "If that's you, here's the link"

**Length:** 300–500 words.

**CTA:** Link to sales page.

**Sample:**

> Three years ago, I was stuck at $4k MRR. I had the product. I had the audience. I had no pricing, no outbound, and no close rate.
>
> The advice I got was generic. The courses I took were vague. I burned 18 months guessing.
>
> Then I rebuilt pricing, outbound, and close rate — the three levers that actually move MRR for B2B SaaS at the $1k–$10k stage. I went from $4k to $50k MRR in 11 months.
>
> I built the 90-Day MRR Sprint to skip the 18 months I wasted.
>
> It's for B2B SaaS founders at $1k–$10k MRR who want to add $30k+ MRR without hiring a sales team. It's not for pre-revenue founders or $50k+ MRR founders — different stage, different playbook.
>
> If that's you: [YES, I'M IN — $7,500]
>
> Doors close Friday.

---

### Email 3 — Quick case study (T-1, morning)

**Subject:** "How [client] added $40k MRR in 11 weeks"

**Body anatomy:**
- 1-paragraph case study with specifics
- Result + timeframe
- "If you're in a similar spot, here's the link"

**Length:** 150–300 words.

**CTA:** Link to sales page.

**Sample:**

> Quick story.
>
> Sarah came in at $3k MRR, burning out on cold email. We rebuilt her pricing (added a $1,500/mo tier), her outbound (switched to warm intros), and her close rate (added a 2-call sequence instead of one).
>
> 11 weeks later: $22k MRR. Same product, same audience, different mechanism.
>
> If you're stuck somewhere similar, the cohort closes Friday: [YES, I WANT THE SPRINT — $7,500]

## Open cart ads (optional)

If running paid traffic to the launch, cart-open day is when ads go live.

**Ad angles:**
- "Just launched: [offer name]" (curiosity)
- "How [client] added $40k MRR" (case study)
- "Add $40k MRR in 90 days" (outcome)

**Targeting:**
- Lookalike of past buyers.
- Lookalike of email list.
- Interest-targeted (B2B SaaS founders, founders, etc.).

**Budget:** ramp over 3–5 days. Don't dump the full budget on day 1.

---

## Open cart list segmentation

Send the right email to the right segment.

| Segment | Email sequence |
|---------|----------------|
| Waitlist members | "Early access: cart opens 24h early" (T-1) + full open cart sequence |
| Engaged subscribers (opened 3+ in last 30 days) | Full open cart sequence |
| Lapsed subscribers (opened in 30–90 days) | Open cart email only (less aggressive) |
| Unengaged (90+ days no open) | Skip launch (or re-engagement sequence) |

Don't send the full launch sequence to subscribers who haven't opened in 6 months. It burns deliverability.

---

## Open cart capacity check

Before cart opens, verify fulfillment capacity.

| Offer type | Capacity check |
|------------|----------------|
| Cohort course | Cap = cohort size. Don't oversell. |
| Self-paced | Unlimited (or whatever the operator caps it at). |
| 1:1 coaching | Cap = number of 1:1 clients operator can serve. |
| Agency project | Cap = team capacity for the engagement. |

**If you oversell:** refund the excess. Don't pretend you can serve 30 buyers with capacity for 20.

## Open cart anti-patterns

### "Cart opens... in 3 days" tease
Announcing cart opens before cart opens kills the urgency. State the date, then send the cart-open email at the date.

### Multiple cart-open emails to non-waitlist
Three cart-open emails to cold subscribers feels pushy. One strong email is better.

### Cart-open without sales page ready
The cart opens and the sales page isn't live. Buyer clicks, sees a 404, leaves.

### Cart-open email with no link
Email says "cart is open" but doesn't link to the sales page. Friction.

### Cart-open email with no scarcity
"Cart is open" without cohort cap or deadline reads as evergreen. Lower urgency.

### Cart-open to unengaged list
Sending the launch sequence to subscribers who haven't opened in 6 months burns sender reputation.

### Cart-open on a Friday afternoon
Lower open rates and conversion on Friday afternoons. Tuesday–Thursday mornings are best.

---

## Open cart timing

**Best cart-open days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday.
**Best cart-open times:** 6–8am in the buyer's timezone, or 10am ET (covers both coasts).

**Worst:**
- Mondays (busy, low attention).
- Fridays (weekend mode starting).
- Saturday/Sunday (lower engagement).
- Major holidays.

**Cart-open duration:**
- PLF: 5–7 days
- Audience launch: 3–5 days
- Signature launch: 7–10 days
- Evergreen: continuous
