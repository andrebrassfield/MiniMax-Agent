# Anti-Patterns Checklist

The full banned-moves list. An offer that ships with any of these is weaker, not stronger. Run this list before the offer goes live.

---

## 1. Fake countdown timers

**What it looks like:**
- "Offer expires in 3:47:12" on an evergreen offer.
- "24 hours left!" that resets every visit.
- "Today only!" repeated weekly.

**Why it's banned:** Buyers can tell. Once they figure out the timer is fake, every other claim on the page loses credibility.

**Replacement:** Real scarcity framework from `scarcity-frameworks.md` — cohort cap, calendar window, evergreen trigger, asset-limited, or partner-limited.

---

## 2. Inflated bonus values

**What it looks like:**
- "$2,997 BONUS!" for a 30-minute template.
- "Total value: $11,458, yours for $497" with values that wouldn't survive scrutiny.
- Stacking 12 bonuses to manufacture a "total value" number.

**Why it's banned:** Buyers do the math. Inflated values train buyers to distrust the offer.

**Replacement:** Real or honestly perceived value per bonus. State the value if the bonus could be sold separately at that price. If it couldn't, don't state a price.

---

## 3. Banned hype words

**The full banned list:**
- `game-changing`
- `revolutionary`
- `secret method`
- `secret sauce`
- `magic`
- `they don't want you to know`
- `one weird trick`
- `breakthrough`
- `unbelievable`
- `insane`
- `mind-blowing`
- `life-changing` (in body copy)
- `unlock`
- `unleash`

**Why they're banned:** These words signal the offer doesn't have substance. Strong offers don't need them. Buyers have heard them too many times — they're white noise.

**Replacement:** Specifics. "Add $40k MRR in 90 days" beats "revolutionary growth system."

---

## 4. Fake scarcity

**What it looks like:**
- "Only 3 spots left!" with no actual cap.
- "Limited spots available" (no specifics).
- "First 10 buyers get..." with no enforcement.

**Why it's banned:** Once buyers find out the scarcity is fake, the offer loses all force.

**Replacement:** Real scarcity from `scarcity-frameworks.md`. State the constraint specifically: "Cohort cap of 12 founders; 7 are taken; next cohort opens in 14 weeks."

---

## 5. Buried terms

**What it looks like:**
- Refund policy in 14 pages of T&Cs.
- Price hidden behind "contact us for pricing" with no anchor.
- Conditions for the guarantee in fine print.

**Why it's banned:** Buried terms signal the operator doesn't want the buyer to see them. Surfaces that lose.

**Replacement:** Surface the price, refund policy, and guarantee conditions on the offer page itself. Buyers who see terms clearly buy more often than buyers who find them later.

---

## 6. Made-up proof

**What it looks like:**
- Testimonials that don't exist.
- Stats with no source ("studies show...").
- "As seen in" with no logo rights.
- Stock-photo headshots next to fake quotes.

**Why it's banned:** Made-up proof is fraud. Even when it's "obviously" exaggerated, it kills the offer's credibility for everyone who notices.

**Replacement:** Real testimonials (with permission), real stats with sources, real logos with rights. If you don't have proof yet, ship without it and build it from the first 10 customers.

---

## 7. Generic outcomes

**What it looks like:**
- "Grow your business"
- "Get healthier"
- "Make more money"
- "Transform your life"
- "Achieve success"

**Why it's banned:** Generic outcomes don't pass the Dream Outcome test. The buyer can't picture themselves on the other side.

**Replacement:** Specific, measurable, ownable outcomes. "Add $40k MRR in 90 days." "Run your first half-marathon in 6 months." "Hire your first 3 engineers."

---

## 8. Bonus stacking theater

**What it looks like:**
- 12 bonuses with no clear purpose for each.
- Bonuses that are old content repurposed.
- Bonuses the buyer wouldn't pay for separately.

**Why it's banned:** Stacking without purpose dilutes the offer. Buyers can tell which bonuses earn their place and which don't.

**Replacement:** 3–6 bonuses, each with a stated reason: "This bonus accelerates time to result / reduces effort / expands outcome / increases perceived likelihood." Cut any bonus that doesn't earn its place.

---

## 9. "Secret method" framing

**What it looks like:**
- "The one method they don't want you to know."
- "Industry secrets revealed."
- "Insider tricks."

**Why it's banned:** "Secret method" frames the offer as adversarial. Most buyers don't want to feel like they're getting away with something — they want to feel like they're making a smart choice.

**Replacement:** Name the method. "The 4-Block Pricing Method" beats "the secret pricing method." Specificity beats secrecy.

---

## 10. Unanchored pricing

**What it looks like:**
- "$5,000" alone with no reference price.
- "Premium pricing for serious buyers."
- "Investment varies" with no anchor.

**Why it's banned:** Unanchored prices read as expensive. Anchored prices read as fair.

**Replacement:** State a reference price (full value, premium tier, or comparison). "$5,000 — vs $25,000 agency engagement" beats "$5,000."

---

## 11. "Risk-free" without specifics

**What it looks like:**
- "100% risk-free!" with no guarantee type stated.
- "Money-back guarantee" without conditions or window.

**Why it's banned:** "Risk-free" without specifics reads as boilerplate. Buyers have seen it too many times.

**Replacement:** Name the guarantee type, conditions, and window. "Conditional 90-day money-back guarantee: complete the work, get the result, or full refund."

---

## 12. Stacked scarcity

**What it looks like:**
- Cohort cap + calendar window + asset-limited + partner-limited + countdown timer, all on the same offer.

**Why it's banned:** Too many constraints compete for attention. Buyers get confused or distrustful.

**Replacement:** One primary scarcity framework. A secondary framework only if it's genuinely true and reinforces the primary.

---

## Quick checklist (use before shipping)

Before the offer goes live, every item must be NO:

- [ ] No fake countdown timers
- [ ] No inflated bonus values
- [ ] No banned hype words in body copy
- [ ] No fake scarcity
- [ ] No buried terms (price, refund, guarantee conditions surfaced)
- [ ] No made-up proof
- [ ] No generic outcomes
- [ ] No bonus stacking theater
- [ ] No "secret method" framing
- [ ] No unanchored pricing
- [ ] No "risk-free" without specifics
- [ ] No stacked scarcity (more than 2 frameworks)

If any item is YES, fix before shipping. Don't ship an offer with anti-patterns and hope nobody notices. They notice.
