# Value Equation — Deep Dive

The Value Equation is the diagnostic. It scores an offer against the four axes the buyer is unconsciously calculating. Use it before designing anything; the score tells you where to invest the design effort.

## The formula

```
Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay × Effort)
```

Numerator = what they get. Denominator = what it costs them. **Maximize numerator. Minimize denominator.**

This isn't a metaphor. It's the buyer's mental model. When the offer under-converts, one of these four is leaking. Find the leak, fix the leak.

---

## Scoring each axis (1–10)

Score the **current** offer against each axis. The leak is the lowest score (or the one the operator can move with most credibility).

### Dream Outcome (numerator)

How vivid, specific, and desirable is the outcome the buyer pictures when they read the offer?

| Score | What it looks like |
|-------|-------------------|
| 1–3 | Generic ("grow your business", "get healthier", "learn marketing"). Buyer can't picture themselves on the other side. |
| 4–6 | Specific but abstract ("land your first 10 customers"). Better, but still leaves the buyer translating. |
| 7–10 | Vivid and ownable ("add $40k MRR in 90 days without hiring a sales team"). Buyer sees the movie. |

**Diagnostic question:** Can the buyer describe the outcome to a friend in one sentence without re-reading the offer?

### Perceived Likelihood (numerator)

How much does the buyer believe THEY will get the result, specifically?

| Score | What it looks like |
|-------|-------------------|
| 1–3 | No proof, no mechanism, no guarantee. "Just trust us." |
| 4–6 | Some proof (logos, testimonials) but generic. "Lots of people got results." |
| 7–10 | Mechanism explained + proof that mirrors the buyer's situation + risk reversal. "People like me got this specific result, here's how, and if I don't, I get my money back." |

**Diagnostic question:** Can the buyer point to a person or situation that looks like them and see the result happening?

### Time Delay (denominator — lower is better)

How long until the buyer sees the result?

| Score | What it looks like |
|-------|-------------------|
| 8–10 | "Lifetime access", "ongoing", "as long as you show up". Open-ended. |
| 4–7 | "Within 6 months", "by end of program". Time-bound but fuzzy. |
| 1–3 | "In 14 days", "first call", "Week 1". Fast and specific. |

**Diagnostic question:** Can the buyer name the day or week they'll see the result?

### Effort (denominator — lower is better)

How hard does the buyer have to work?

| Score | What it looks like |
|-------|-------------------|
| 8–10 | "10 hrs/week of homework", "you must complete all 47 modules", "bring your own materials". |
| 4–7 | "2 hrs/week", "weekly calls + light homework". Reasonable. |
| 1–3 | "Show up to 4 calls", "we do the work for you", "15 min/day". Low effort. |

**Diagnostic question:** Does the buyer have to do anything they don't already do?

---

## Common leak patterns

These are the four offers I see most often. The leak is always in the same place.

| Pattern | Where it leaks | Fix |
|---------|---------------|-----|
| **Expert with no specificity** | Dream Outcome is generic. "I help businesses grow." | Make the outcome specific and ownable. One sentence the buyer can repeat. |
| **Good product, no proof** | Perceived Likelihood is 3. "Just trust the process." | Add mechanism + matching proof + risk reversal. |
| **Slow result, high effort** | Time Delay × Effort is huge. "Master this in 12 months with 10 hrs/week." | Compress the timeline OR reduce the effort OR raise the price to make the effort feel worth it. |
| **High effort, vague outcome** | All four are mediocre. | Almost always needs a reframe — the offer is positioned wrong, not designed wrong. |

## Worked example

**Input:** Coach selling "1:1 business coaching" at $5,000/month. Three-month commitment. "I'll help you scale your business."

| Axis | Score | Why |
|------|-------|-----|
| Dream Outcome | 3 | "Scale your business" — generic. Buyer can't picture it. |
| Perceived Likelihood | 4 | Some testimonials, but no mechanism explained, no risk reversal. |
| Time Delay | 7 | "Three months" — fuzzy on when results show. |
| Effort | 5 | "1 hr/week" — reasonable but not specified. |

**Leak:** Dream Outcome (lowest score).

**Fix direction:** Get specific on outcome. "Add $30k MRR in 90 days by fixing your pricing and outbound." Now Dream Outcome jumps to 8 — the buyer sees the movie.

Re-score after fix:

| Axis | Before | After |
|------|--------|-------|
| Dream Outcome | 3 | 8 |
| Perceived Likelihood | 4 | 5 (unchanged — still no risk reversal) |
| Time Delay | 7 | 5 ("in 90 days" is specific) |
| Effort | 5 | 5 (unchanged) |

**Value score** (before/after, ratio): 3×4 / (7×5) = 12/35 = **0.34** → 8×5 / (5×5) = 40/25 = **1.60**. **4.7x lift** on the Value Equation score, before changing any copy.

That's the leverage. Fix the offer, the copy writes itself.

---

## When to re-score

Re-score after each component change in Stage 3. The score should climb. If it doesn't, the component didn't move the leak — redesign.

Final re-score before the anti-pattern gate in Stage 5. The offer ships when the score is high AND no axis is below 4.
