# Before / After Teardowns — 6 Anonymized Examples

Six real (anonymized) before/after pairs. Each shows: business type, broken offer → fixed offer, what changed, why it works.

The examples are anonymized but representative. Patterns repeat across industries.

---

## Teardown 1 — B2B Consultant

### Before

> **Offer:** "I help B2B SaaS companies improve their sales process."
> **Price:** $15,000/month, 6-month minimum.
> **Components:** Weekly 60-min call, async support.

### Why it was leaking

- Dream Outcome: 3/10 ("improve sales process" — generic).
- Perceived Likelihood: 3/10 (no proof, no guarantee).
- Time Delay: 6/10 (results in 6 months).
- Effort: 5/10 (calls + homework unspecified).

Conversion was stuck at ~5% on warm leads.

### After

> **Offer:** "The 90-Day Pipeline Rebuild — we get your B2B SaaS team from 30% → 60% SQL-to-opportunity conversion in 90 days, or you don't pay for the engagement."
> **Price:** $25,000 for 90 days (anchored against "hiring a VP of Sales at $250k+").
> **Components:**
> - Core: 12 weekly calls + async reviews + a written sales playbook by Day 30.
> - Bonus: Sales-call audit template ($497), outreach swipe file ($297).
> - Guarantee: Conditional performance guarantee on the 60% SQL-to-opp metric.
> - Scarcity: "I take 4 clients per quarter."
> - Name: "The 90-Day Pipeline Rebuild".

### What changed

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| Generic outcome | Specific outcome with metric |
| No guarantee | Conditional performance guarantee |
| Soft pricing | Anchored, justified, higher |
| No name | Named, outcome-shaped |

Conversion moved to ~25% on warm leads. Same traffic. Same operator. Different offer.

## Teardown 2 — Course Creator

### Before

> **Offer:** "The Marketing Course — 47 modules, 12 live calls, lifetime access."
> **Price:** $997.

### Why it was leaking

- Curriculum-shaped offer. Buyer reads "47 modules" and bounces.
- Lifetime access = no urgency.
- No bonus stack, no guarantee stated.
- "Marketing Course" is generic.

### After

> **Offer:** "The 30-Day First Customers — get 10 paying customers in 30 days without paid ads. Cohort-based, 8 weeks total."
> **Price:** $1,497 one-time or 3 × $549.
> **Components:**
> - Core: 6 modules (specific), 8 weekly calls, written customer-acquisition plan by Day 14.
> - Bonus: Outreach swipe file (50 templates, $297 value), weekly office hours (included), alumni community access (included).
> - Guarantee: Rolling-cohort — "if you do the work and don't have 10 paying customers by Day 30, join the next cohort free".
> - Scarcity: Cohort cap of 30. Next cohort opens in 8 weeks.
> - Name: "The 30-Day First Customers".

### What changed

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| 47 modules | 6 named modules |
| Lifetime access | Time-bound (30 days) |
| No guarantee | Rolling-cohort guarantee |
| Generic name | Outcome-shaped name |
| $997 (unanchored) | $1,497 anchored against customer LTV |

Same operator, same content. Different offer.

## Teardown 3 — Coach

### Before

> **Offer:** "1:1 coaching with me, weekly calls, async support. $8,000 for 3 months."
> **No defined deliverable, no guarantee, no scarcity, generic positioning.**

### Why it was leaking

- Buyer can't picture the deliverable.
- $8k is unanchored.
- No risk reversal at high ticket.
- Operator takes unlimited clients → no scarcity.

### After

> **Offer:** "Founder Clarity Intensive — over 12 weeks, we define your offer, price, and go-to-market motion. You leave with a written playbook, a named offer that passes the Value Equation, and a 90-day execution plan."
> **Price:** $12,000 for 12 weeks.
> **Components:**
> - Core: 8 bi-weekly 75-min calls + async Loom reviews + written offer spec + written 90-day plan by Week 12.
> - Bonus: "Pricing diagnostic" template ($497), "GTM motion decision tree" ($297).
> - Guarantee: Conditional — "complete all 8 calls and the homework and don't have an offer that passes the Value Equation by Week 12, full refund".
> - Scarcity: "I take 4 founders per quarter."
> - Name: "Founder Clarity Intensive".

### What changed

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| Vague "coaching" | Named deliverable (offer spec, GTM plan) |
| Unanchored $8k | $12k anchored against "hiring a strategy consultant for $30k+" |
| No guarantee | Conditional guarantee aligned to outcome |
| No scarcity | Capacity limit (4 founders/quarter) |

Same coach. Same expertise. Different offer.

## Teardown 4 — Agency

### Before

> **Offer:** "We help e-commerce brands grow. Retainer: $5,000/month."
> **No named deliverables, no reporting cadence, no risk-sharing.**

### Why it was leaking

- "Help grow" is generic.
- $5k/month unanchored.
- Operator's risk is zero regardless of outcome.

### After

> **Offer:** "The 90-Day Storefront Sprint — we rebuild your e-commerce site, set up your email automation, and launch 3 campaigns in 90 days. Reporting weekly. Outcome: $50k in attributable revenue from launch campaigns, or month 3 is free."
> **Price:** $18,000 for 90 days (or $7,000/month).
> **Components:**
> - Core: Site rebuild, email automation setup, 3 campaign launches, weekly reporting.
> - Bonus: Customer-acquisition playbook the brand can use internally ($997 value).
> - Guarantee: Performance — month 3 free if $50k attributable revenue not hit.
> - Scarcity: "We work with 6 brands at a time."
> - Name: "The 90-Day Storefront Sprint".

### What changed

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| Generic "grow" | Specific 90-day scope with named deliverables |
| $5k/month unanchored | $18k for 90 days, anchored against $30k+ agency comparison |
| Zero operator risk | Performance guarantee (month 3 free) |
| No scarcity | Capacity limit (6 brands) |

Same agency team. Same capabilities. Different offer.

## Teardown 5 — Info Product

### Before

> **Offer:** "The Productivity Bundle — 4 courses + 12 templates + 3 workbooks + 7 swipe files. Total value: $4,997. Yours for $497."
> **Bonus inflation theater, no real scarcity, generic positioning.**

### Why it was leaking

- "Productivity Bundle" — no specific outcome.
- Inflated total value math ($4,997 of assets that wouldn't sell for $500).
- Buyers can smell the inflation.
- $497 sits there alone with no anchor.

### After

> **Offer:** "The Async Operator's Toolkit — 12 templates + 4 workbooks that turn your calendar into a 25-hour workweek. Self-paced. Lifetime access."
> **Price:** $297 (anchored against "$2,000+ in 1:1 productivity coaching").
> **Components:**
> - Core: 12 templates, 4 workbooks. Specifically named.
> - Bonus: 30-min video walkthrough of each template (real value, not inflated).
> - Guarantee: 60-day unconditional money-back.
> - Scarcity: None (evergreen) — accepted lower conversion for scalability.
> - Name: "The Async Operator's Toolkit".

### What changed

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| Bundled everything | Curated specifically for async operators |
| Inflated values | Real value stated honestly |
| $497 alone | $297 anchored against $2,000+ |
| Bonus inflation | Removed bonus theater; made core stronger |

Conversion went up. Refund rate went down. Same products. Different offer.

## Teardown 6 — High-Ticket B2B

### Before

> **Offer:** "We provide enterprise data integration services. Contact us for pricing."
> **Generic, no anchor, no specificity on what gets delivered.**

### Why it was leaking

- "Enterprise data integration services" — could be anything.
- "Contact us for pricing" reads as unpriced = expensive.
- No defined phases, deliverables, or success criteria.

### After

> **Offer:** "The 90-Day Data Integration Pilot — we integrate your Salesforce, NetSuite, and Snowflake environments in 90 days, with bi-weekly milestone reviews and a documented handoff. Pilot fee: $75,000, credited toward the annual contract if you continue."
> **Components:**
> - Core: 4 named phases with deliverables, bi-weekly milestone reviews, documented handoff.
> - Bonus: Internal team's training session (4 hours, $5,000 value).
> - Guarantee: Milestone-based — at Day 60, if integration is off-track, you can exit with 50% refund.
> - Scarcity: None (enterprise sales process).
> - Name: "The 90-Day Data Integration Pilot".

### What changed

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| Generic capability | Specific 90-day pilot with named systems |
| "Contact us" | $75k pilot fee (with credit toward annual) |
| No milestones | Bi-weekly reviews + exit option at Day 60 |
| Vague positioning | "Pilot" frame (de-risks for buyer, qualifies for operator) |

Same firm, same capabilities. Different offer.

## What the teardowns have in common

Across all six:

1. **Specificity beats generality.** Every before had a generic outcome. Every after named a specific outcome with a metric.
2. **Anchoring beats unanchored pricing.** Every before had a naked price. Every after had a reference price or comparison.
3. **Guarantee beats no guarantee.** Every before had no risk reversal. Every after had a guarantee aligned to the outcome.
4. **Naming beats generic naming.** Every before had a generic or unnamed offer. Every after had an outcome-shaped name.
5. **Scarcity (real) beats unlimited.** Most befores had unlimited capacity. Most afters had a real constraint.

The patterns are consistent. The lift from "before" to "after" is consistently 2–5x conversion on the same traffic.

---

## How to use these teardowns

When diagnosing an operator's offer:

1. Find the closest analog in this list.
2. Note what changed from before → after.
3. Apply the same patterns to the operator's offer.
4. Re-score the Value Equation before designing.

The teardowns aren't templates to copy. They're patterns to apply. Each operator's specifics drive the final offer.
