# Empathy Pivot Template — x-empowerment-hunter

The 3-beat structure every Aggressive Empathy reply follows. The
Scribe fills in this template with source-post-specific details,
not a copy of the template.

## The 3-beat structure

| Beat | Purpose | Length | Example opener |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Acknowledge the fear | Mirror the person's actual concern. Set the tone as peer-to-peer, not coach-to-noob. | 1 sentence, 30-50 chars | "I hear you on this." / "This is a real fear." / "You're not wrong to be worried." |
| 2. Reframe the threat | The threat isn't the AI; it's the coworker who's already using it. Or: the threat isn't the tool; it's the workflow that hasn't been touched. | 1 sentence, 50-90 chars | "The threat isn't the AI, it's the coworker who's already using it." |
| 3. Tactical play | Specific tool + specific task + specific time window. The load-bearing element. | 2-3 sentences, 100-150 chars | "Open X. Do Y. Walk into Z." |

**Total:** 180-290 chars. Hard cap 280.

## Skeleton (NOT a real draft)

> "I hear you on this. The threat isn't the AI, it's the coworker who's already using it. Open ChatGPT this weekend. Paste in your last 4 weekly status reports. Ask it to write the 5th one in your voice. Walk into Monday with 90 minutes pre-done."

The Scribe's job is to fill in this skeleton with source-post-specific
details, not to copy the template verbatim. Different anxieties
need different reframes and different tactical plays.

## What makes a good tactical play

The tactical play is the load-bearing element. A good tactical
play is:
- **Specific** (named tool, named task, named time window)
- **Boring** (the person can do it this weekend without learning anything new)
- **Concrete** (the person knows they've done something specific, not "learned about AI")
- **Verifiable** (the person can check they did it — "did I open ChatGPT?" is a yes/no)

**Good tactical play examples:**
- "Open ChatGPT this weekend. Paste in your last 4 weekly status reports. Ask it to write the 5th one in your voice. Walk into Monday with 90 minutes pre-done."
- "Spend 30 minutes on Friday writing a single Zapier: 'new row in spreadsheet → SMS to me.' That's a 6-line automation. You just bought yourself a 2-hour morning."
- "Open your CRM right now. Filter for deals you lost in the last 90 days. Open 5 of them. Read what the prospect actually said. That's the workflow AI can't replace — knowing the buyer."

**Bad tactical play examples (the preachy floor violations):**
- "Learn AI tools." (vague)
- "Stay ahead of the curve." (vague)
- "Upskill in the latest AI." (corporate coach)
- "The future belongs to those who adapt." (LinkedIn influencer)
- "You should learn prompt engineering." (condescending)

## The empathy floor (the load-bearing quality check)

The Scribe's draft MUST open with empathy (Beat 1). If the Scribe
returns a draft that opens with:
- "Don't worry..." (argues with the fear — violation)
- "AI won't take your job..." (argues with the fear — violation)
- "Just learn AI..." (preachy — violation)
- "You need to adapt..." (preachy — violation)
- "The future is bright..." (LinkedIn influencer — violation)
- "Here's why you're wrong..." (argues with the fear — violation)
- "I disagree..." (argues — violation)
- "You're overreacting..." (invalidates the fear — violation)

...then the empathy floor is violated. Halt and surface for the
Scribe to retry with a proper Beat 1.

## The preachy floor (the other quality check)

Even with a proper Beat 1, the Scribe's draft MUST NOT read like
a corporate coach or LinkedIn influencer. The preachy floor catches:
- "You should..." (advisory tone — violation)
- "You need to..." (advisory tone — violation)
- "The future is..." (LinkedIn voice — violation)
- "It's important to..." (academic register — violation)
- "I will show you..." (coach tone — violation)
- "Let me teach you..." (coach tone — violation)

The reply is peer-to-peer. Speak as a peer who has done the tactical
play themselves, not as a coach explaining it.

## Total: 3 floors (empathy, preachy, banned phrases)

The Scribe's draft must pass all 3 floors before returning:
1. **Empathy floor** — opens with acknowledging the fear
2. **Preachy floor** — does not read like a corporate coach
3. **Banned-phrases floor** — re-grep against the Scribe's persona spec

Failure at any floor → halt and surface for the Scribe to retry.
