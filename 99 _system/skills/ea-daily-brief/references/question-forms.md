# Question Forms — ea-daily-brief

The closing question is the load-bearing element of the brief. Per
EA contract behavior #3, the brief ends with a QUESTION, not a
task. The question must:
- Be answerable in 1-2 sentences by Andre
- Force a decision or a prioritization
- Reference a specific surface from the brief (so Andre can drill in)

## Good question forms

**Decision-forcing:**
- "Ship the ea-research-brief regulatory anchors as-is, or hold for
  the GEPA review?"
- "Is the Mavis ↔ Hermes mirror a working surface or a hard
  boundary?"
- "Which of these 3 connections is the one you want me to chase?"

**Prioritization-forcing:**
- "Should the next Scribe batch focus on Pillar 4 (P4 has 0 production
  posts so far) or stay on Pillar 5 (P5 has 6 used hooks, the data
  says it's working)?"
- "Of the 2 contradictions flagged, do you want to resolve
  hermes-co-processor first or the Mavis EA scope boundary?"

**Drill-in forcing:**
- "The skill-mirror sync drift is showing up in 2 of 3 recent skill
  creations — run the mirror-sync audit now, or batch it into the
  weekly?"

## Bad question forms (halt and rewrite)

- **"What do you want me to do?"** — too open, no decision forced
- **"Should I keep going?"** — status check, not decision
- **"Want me to file these?"** — task, not question
- **"Is there anything else you need?"** — empty, no specific surface
- **"How does this look?"** — feedback request, not decision

## The rewrite test

If the closing line starts with "what," "should I," or "do you want
me to" — it's a task-asking question, not a decision-forcing one.
Rewrite using a good form above.

## When there's no real question

If the corpus surfaces no decision Andre needs to make today, the
brief's question can be a **prioritization** ("which connection to
chase") or a **scope probe** ("is X still in scope?"). A "no
question" brief is allowed on quiet days — but the question is the
load-bearing element when the corpus has signal.
